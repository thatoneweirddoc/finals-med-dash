## MSK & Rheumatology Pharmacology & Prescribing


### MSK & Rheumatology Notes — MSK & Rheumatology Pharmacology & Prescribing

#### 8.1 Analgesia & anti-inflammatories

**NSAIDs** — inhibit **cyclo-oxygenase (COX)** → less prostaglandin → analgesia/anti-inflammation.
- ⚑ **The three big harms:** **GI** (ulcer/bleed — co-prescribe a **PPI** for at-risk patients), **renal** (AKI, fluid retention — avoid in CKD/hypovolaemia; part of the nephrotoxic "triple whammy" with ACEi + diuretic), **cardiovascular** (raised MI/stroke risk, esp. high-dose diclofenac and COX-2 selectives). Also bronchospasm in aspirin-sensitive asthma.
- **COX-2 selectives (celecoxib, etoricoxib):** less GI risk, but **cardiovascular risk persists**; avoid in established cardiovascular disease.

**Corticosteroids** — potent anti-inflammatory/immunosuppressant; oral (prednisolone), **intra-articular** (flares of OA/RA/crystal), IV pulsed (severe vasculitis/GCA visual loss).
- ⚑ **Harms of chronic use:** osteoporosis (co-prescribe bone protection with long courses), hyperglycaemia, hypertension, weight gain/Cushingoid, infection, adrenal suppression (**never stop abruptly — sick-day rules**), cataracts, skin thinning, mood.

#### 8.2 DMARDs (disease-modifying antirheumatic drugs)

##### Conventional synthetic (csDMARDs)

| Drug | Notes / monitoring | Key toxicity |
|---|---|---|
| **Methotrexate** ⚑ | **Anchor DMARD for RA**; weekly; **co-prescribe folic acid**; FBC/LFT/U&E monitoring | **Myelosuppression, hepatotoxicity, pneumonitis; teratogenic** (stop ≥3–6 months pre-conception, both sexes advised); avoid with trimethoprim (marrow) |
| **Sulfasalazine** | Useful in RA and peripheral spondyloarthropathy; relatively pregnancy-safe | Rash, GI upset, reversible ↓sperm, myelosuppression |
| **Hydroxychloroquine** | RA (mild), **SLE backbone** | ⚑ **Retinopathy → baseline + annual retinal screening**; relatively pregnancy-safe |
| **Leflunomide** | RA/PsA | Hypertension, hepatotoxicity, **teratogenic (long half-life — needs washout)** |

##### Biologic (bDMARDs) — the cytokine/cell targets

| Class | Examples | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **TNF-α inhibitors** | infliximab, adalimumab, etanercept, golimumab | RA, axSpA, PsA, IBD — ⚑ **screen for latent TB and hepatitis B before starting** (reactivation) |
| **IL-6 inhibitor** | **tocilizumab** | RA, **GCA**, JIA |
| **Anti-CD20 (B cell)** | rituximab | RA, **ANCA vasculitis**, SLE |
| **T-cell co-stim** | abatacept | RA |
| **IL-17 / IL-23** | secukinumab / ustekinumab, guselkumab | **PsA, axSpA, psoriasis** |
| **Anti-BLyS** | belimumab | SLE |

##### Targeted synthetic (tsDMARDs)
- **JAK inhibitors** (tofacitinib, baricitinib, upadacitinib) — oral, block intracellular JAK–STAT.
- ⚑ **Safety signal (ORAL Surveillance):** increased **MACE, VTE, serious infection (incl. herpes zoster) and malignancy** vs TNF inhibitors — reserved for patients who fail/can't take a TNF inhibitor, and used cautiously in **≥65 yrs, smokers and those with cardiovascular/thrombotic risk**.

#### 8.3 Gout, bone & other targeted agents

##### Gout drugs *(the acute-vs-chronic split is the exam point)*

| Phase | Drugs |
|---|---|
| **Acute attack** | **NSAID**, **colchicine** (⚑ low-dose regimen now; caution in renal impairment — diarrhoea is the dose-limiting effect), **corticosteroid** (oral or intra-articular — good when NSAID/colchicine contraindicated) |
| **Urate-lowering (ULT)** | **Allopurinol** (xanthine oxidase inhibitor) — ⚑ **start low (100 mg/day; 50 mg if CKD) and titrate to target urate <0.36 mmol/L (<0.30 if tophi)**; cover initiation with **NSAID/colchicine prophylaxis 3–6 months**; ⚑ **HLA-B*5801 testing in high-risk ethnicities** (SJS/TEN risk). **Febuxostat** second-line. **Don't start ULT during an acute flare, and don't stop it if already established.** |

##### Bone-targeted agents

| Drug | Mechanism | Key point |
|---|---|---|
| **Bisphosphonates** (alendronate, risedronate, IV zoledronate) | Inhibit osteoclasts | ⚑ Oral: **take fasting, upright, with water, stay upright 30 min** (oesophagitis); rare **osteonecrosis of the jaw / atypical femoral fracture**; renal caution |
| **Denosumab** | **Anti-RANKL** monoclonal | 6-monthly SC; ⚑ **rebound vertebral fractures if stopped** → transition to bisphosphonate; hypocalcaemia risk |
| **Romosozumab** ⚑ | **Anti-sclerostin** (anabolic) | Builds bone; cardiovascular caution |
| **Teriparatide** | **PTH analogue** (anabolic) | Daily SC, limited duration |

##### Other
- **Neuropathic/central pain adjuncts** (amitriptyline, duloxetine, pregabalin) for fibromyalgia and neuropathic MSK pain; **NSAIDs/steroids do not help fibromyalgia**.

#### 8.4 Prescribing safety & shared care

- **Before starting biologics/DMARDs:** screen for **latent TB, hepatitis B/C, HIV**; check vaccinations (⚑ **no live vaccines on biologics/high-dose immunosuppression**); baseline FBC/LFT/U&E.
- **Monitoring:** regular FBC/LFT/U&E on methotrexate/leflunomide/sulfasalazine/azathioprine (shared-care bloods); annual retinal screen on hydroxychloroquine.
- **Steroids:** bone protection + PPI on long courses; **sick-day rules**; taper — never stop abruptly.
- **Pregnancy:** ⚑ **safe(r): hydroxychloroquine, sulfasalazine, azathioprine, low-dose prednisolone, certolizumab**; **teratogenic/avoid: methotrexate, leflunomide, cyclophosphamide, mycophenolate, and (in APS) warfarin in the first trimester** (switch to LMWH).
- ⚑ **Azathioprine + allopurinol** is a dangerous combination (allopurinol blocks azathioprine metabolism → marrow toxicity); check **TPMT** before azathioprine.
