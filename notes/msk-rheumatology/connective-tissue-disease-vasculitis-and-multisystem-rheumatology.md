## Connective Tissue Disease, Vasculitis & Multisystem Rheumatology


### MSK & Rheumatology Notes — Connective Tissue Disease, Vasculitis & Multisystem Rheumatology

Thresholds/guideline items cross-checked ≥2 sources; changed guidance flagged ⚑; discriminators **bolded**.*

#### 6.1 Systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) & antiphospholipid syndrome

##### SLE
A **multisystem autoimmune immune-complex disease**, classically a **young woman of reproductive age** (higher prevalence and severity in Afro-Caribbean and Asian populations).

**Clinical (any system):** constitutional (fatigue, fever, weight loss); **malar "butterfly" rash** (spares nasolabial folds), photosensitivity, discoid lesions, oral ulcers, non-scarring alopecia; **non-erosive arthritis** (small joints — vs RA which erodes); serositis (pleuritis, pericarditis); **lupus nephritis** (the major prognostic determinant); neuropsychiatric lupus; haematological (haemolytic anaemia, leucopenia, lymphopenia, thrombocytopenia); Raynaud's.

**Immunology (see also Section 3):** **ANA** (sensitive screen — the **entry criterion**), **anti-dsDNA** (specific, tracks activity/nephritis), **anti-Sm** (specific), anti-Ro/La, **low C3/C4 in active disease**.

⚑ **2019 EULAR/ACR classification criteria:** **entry criterion = ANA ≥1:80** (must be positive to classify), then **weighted additive criteria** across clinical and immunological domains (only the highest-weighted item per domain counts; a criterion doesn't count if better explained by another cause); **≥10 points** classifies SLE. High-weight items include **lupus nephritis on biopsy** and **anti-dsDNA/anti-Sm**. (Classification, not a diagnostic checklist — but ANA-as-entry is the examinable change.)

**Lupus nephritis:** class determined by **renal biopsy** (ISN/RPS I–VI; **class III/IV proliferative** is the aggressive form needing immunosuppression). Screen everyone with **urinalysis + urine protein:creatinine ratio + BP**.

**Management:** ⚑ **hydroxychloroquine for essentially all patients** (reduces flares, organ damage, thrombosis, mortality — needs annual retinal screening); glucocorticoids for flares (minimise long-term); immunosuppression for organ disease (**mycophenolate mofetil or cyclophosphamide + steroids** for proliferative lupus nephritis, increasingly **plus belimumab or voclosporin** ⚑); sun protection; cardiovascular risk management.

**Drug-induced lupus:** **anti-histone antibodies**; culprits **hydralazine, procainamide, isoniazid, minocycline**; spares kidney/CNS, resolves on stopping the drug.

##### Antiphospholipid syndrome (APS)
Autoantibody-mediated **hypercoagulable state** — primary or secondary to SLE.
- **Clinical: arterial and venous Thrombosis + pregnancy morbidity** (recurrent miscarriage, late fetal loss), thrombocytopenia, livedo reticularis. **Memory hook — "CLOT":** **C**oagulation defect, **L**ivedo reticularis, **O**bstetric loss, **T**hrombocytopenia.
- **Antibodies:** **lupus anticoagulant, anticardiolipin, anti-β2-glycoprotein-I** (need persistence ≥12 weeks apart).
- ⚑ **Paradox:** the lupus anticoagulant **prolongs the APTT in vitro** but causes **thrombosis in vivo**.
- **Management:** long-term **warfarin** for thrombosis (⚑ **DOACs are inferior/avoided, especially in triple-positive/arterial APS**); **aspirin + LMWH** in pregnancy.

#### 6.2 Systemic sclerosis, Sjögren's & inflammatory myopathies

##### Systemic sclerosis (scleroderma)
Excess collagen → skin and internal-organ **fibrosis** + **vasculopathy**. Two forms — the split is high-yield:

| | **Limited cutaneous** (incl. **CREST**) | **Diffuse cutaneous** |
|---|---|---|
| Skin | Face + distal to elbows/knees | Proximal + trunk |
| Antibody | **Anti-centromere** | **Anti-Scl-70 (topoisomerase)** (also anti-RNA-pol III) |
| Onset | Long-standing Raynaud's first | Rapid |
| Big risk | **Pulmonary arterial hypertension** | **Interstitial lung disease; scleroderma renal crisis** |

**CREST = C**alcinosis, **R**aynaud's, o**E**sophageal dysmotility, **S**clerodactyly, **T**elangiectasia.
⚑ **Scleroderma renal crisis** — acute severe hypertension + AKI (diffuse disease, worsened by high-dose steroids) → **ACE inhibitor** is the treatment (one of the few times you push ACEi in an AKI). ILD → screen with PFTs/HRCT; treat with mycophenolate ± nintedanib.

##### Sjögren's syndrome
**Lymphocytic infiltration of exocrine glands** → **sicca (dry eyes = keratoconjunctivitis sicca, dry mouth = xerostomia)**, parotid swelling; primary or secondary (to RA/SLE).
- **Antibodies: anti-Ro (SSA), anti-La (SSB)**, RF, ANA; **Schirmer's test**, salivary gland biopsy.
- ⚑ **Increased risk of B-cell non-Hodgkin (MALT) lymphoma** — new persistent parotid swelling warrants investigation.

##### Inflammatory myopathies (polymyositis / dermatomyositis)
- **Symmetrical proximal muscle weakness** (difficulty rising from a chair, combing hair), **markedly raised CK**, EMG/muscle biopsy changes, **anti-Jo-1** (antisynthetase syndrome → ILD, mechanic's hands, fever).
- **Dermatomyositis** adds skin: **heliotrope rash** (violaceous eyelids), **Gottron's papules** (knuckles), shawl sign. ⚑ **Strong association with underlying malignancy (esp. dermatomyositis in adults)** → **malignancy screen**.
- Management: high-dose steroids + steroid-sparing immunosuppression.

##### Mixed connective tissue disease (MCTD)
Overlap of SLE + scleroderma + myositis features with **high-titre anti-U1-RNP**; Raynaud's, puffy hands.

#### 6.3 Large- and medium-vessel vasculitis

##### Giant cell arteritis (GCA) *(emergency — sight-threatening)* ⚑
Granulomatous **large-vessel vasculitis** in the **over-50s**, affecting branches of the external carotid.
- **Clinical: new headache (temporal), scalp tenderness, jaw claudication** (highly suggestive), **visual disturbance/amaurosis fugax → permanent monocular blindness** (anterior ischaemic optic neuropathy), temporal artery tenderness/thickening/pulselessness; **raised ESR (often >50) and CRP**; ~40–50% overlap with **polymyalgia rheumatica**.
- **Investigation:** ESR/CRP, **temporal artery biopsy** (granulomatous inflammation, giant cells — ⚑ **skip lesions → a negative biopsy does not exclude it**), and **temporal artery ultrasound "halo sign"** (increasingly a first-line, biopsy-sparing test in fast-track pathways).
- ⚑ **Management: start high-dose glucocorticoids IMMEDIATELY on clinical suspicion — do NOT wait for the biopsy** (biopsy stays positive for ~1–2 weeks after starting steroids). **Visual symptoms → IV methylprednisolone; no visual symptoms → high-dose oral prednisolone (~40–60 mg).** Add **low-dose aspirin**; **tocilizumab (IL-6 inhibitor)** as a **steroid-sparing agent** (2021 ACR/VF) for relapsing or high-steroid-burden disease. Bone/GI protection with long steroid course.

##### Polymyalgia rheumatica (PMR)
- **Bilateral shoulder and pelvic-girdle pain and stiffness** in the over-50s, **profound early-morning stiffness**, raised ESR/CRP; **NO true muscle weakness** (pain limits, CK normal — the discriminator from myositis).
- **Dramatic response to low-dose prednisolone (~15 mg)** — a near-diagnostic feature. Overlaps with GCA; always **ask about GCA symptoms** in a PMR patient.

##### Takayasu arteritis & polyarteritis nodosa
- **Takayasu** ("pulseless disease") — large-vessel (aorta/branches) vasculitis in **young Asian women**; absent pulses, BP discrepancy, bruits, claudication.
- **Polyarteritis nodosa (PAN)** — **medium-vessel** necrotising vasculitis; **spares the lungs**; **hepatitis B** association; renal, GI, skin, mononeuritis multiplex; **"rosary-bead" microaneurysms** on angiography; **ANCA-negative**.

#### 6.4 Small-vessel (ANCA-associated) vasculitis & others

**ANCA-associated vasculitides (AAV)** — small-vessel, the **pulmonary–renal** disease group:

| Disease | ANCA | Signature |
|---|---|---|
| **Granulomatosis with polyangiitis (GPA)** (Wegener's) | **c-ANCA / PR3** | **ENT (saddle-nose, sinusitis, otitis), lungs (nodules/haemorrhage), kidney (RPGN)**; the "**E-L-K**" trio |
| **Eosinophilic granulomatosis with polyangiitis (EGPA)** (Churg–Strauss) | **p-ANCA / MPO** (~40%) | **Asthma + eosinophilia + vasculitis**; nasal polyps, mononeuritis multiplex |
| **Microscopic polyangiitis (MPA)** | **p-ANCA / MPO** | **Pulmonary–renal** (glomerulonephritis + alveolar haemorrhage), no granulomas |

- **Presentation clues:** constitutional symptoms + **glomerulonephritis (blood + protein + red-cell casts)** ± **pulmonary haemorrhage** (haemoptysis) + **mononeuritis multiplex** + skin purpura/ENT.
- ⚑ **Management (2021 ACR/VF):** **remission induction with rituximab (or cyclophosphamide) + high-dose glucocorticoids** (± plasma exchange for severe renal/pulmonary haemorrhage); then maintenance (rituximab/azathioprine). A rheumatology/nephrology emergency when rapidly progressive.

**Other vasculitides / mimics:**
- **IgA vasculitis (Henoch–Schönlein purpura)** — children after URTI: **palpable purpura (buttocks/legs) + arthralgia + abdominal pain + IgA nephropathy**; usually self-limiting.
- **Behçet's disease** — **recurrent oral + genital ulcers + uveitis**, pathergy, Silk-Road distribution; variable-vessel.
- **Cryoglobulinaemic vasculitis** — hepatitis C association; purpura, arthralgia, neuropathy, low C4.

#### 6.5 Other rheumatology worth knowing

##### Fibromyalgia
**Chronic widespread pain** with fatigue, unrefreshing sleep, cognitive "fog", multiple tender points — **normal inflammatory markers, normal imaging, no synovitis**. ⚑ **The key contrast:** it is a **non-inflammatory** central pain-sensitisation syndrome — the giveaway is *lots of symptoms, normal bloods*. Management is non-pharmacological first (graded exercise, CBT, education), then adjuncts (amitriptyline, duloxetine, pregabalin); **NSAIDs/steroids don't help**.

##### Others in brief
- **Adult-onset Still's disease** — spiking (quotidian) fevers, **salmon-pink evanescent rash**, arthritis, sore throat, **very high ferritin**, raised WCC, seronegative.
- **Sarcoid arthropathy** — **Löfgren's syndrome** (erythema nodosum + bilateral hilar lymphadenopathy + ankle arthritis) is the acute, good-prognosis form.
- **Juvenile idiopathic arthritis (JIA)** — arthritis <16 yrs, ≥6 weeks; subtypes (oligoarticular — **uveitis risk, ANA-positive**; polyarticular; **systemic/Still's**); watch for uveitis screening.

#### Rapid-Recall Discriminators — CTD & Vasculitis

| Clue / question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Young woman, malar rash, non-erosive arthritis, ANA + | **SLE** |
| Specific SLE antibodies / activity marker | **Anti-dsDNA, anti-Sm** / **low C3/C4** |
| Prolonged APTT + thrombosis + miscarriage | **Antiphospholipid syndrome** |
| Anti-histone antibodies after hydralazine | **Drug-induced lupus** |
| Anti-centromere / limited scleroderma risk | **CREST** / pulmonary hypertension |
| Anti-Scl-70 / diffuse scleroderma risks | ILD + **scleroderma renal crisis** (Rx **ACE inhibitor**) |
| Dry eyes + dry mouth + anti-Ro/La; lymphoma risk | **Sjögren's** |
| Proximal weakness + raised CK + heliotrope rash | **Dermatomyositis** (+ malignancy screen) |
| High-titre anti-U1-RNP | **Mixed connective tissue disease** |
| >50, headache, jaw claudication, ESR >50, visual loss | **Giant cell arteritis** → **immediate steroids** ⚑ |
| Shoulder/pelvic-girdle stiffness, normal CK, steroid-responsive | **Polymyalgia rheumatica** |
| c-ANCA/PR3, saddle nose + lung + kidney | **GPA** |
| Asthma + eosinophilia + vasculitis (p-ANCA) | **EGPA (Churg–Strauss)** |
| Medium-vessel, spares lungs, hepatitis B, ANCA-negative | **Polyarteritis nodosa** |
| Child, palpable purpura on buttocks + abdo pain + haematuria | **IgA vasculitis (HSP)** |
| Widespread pain, fatigue, **normal bloods and imaging** | **Fibromyalgia** |
| Salmon-pink rash, spiking fever, sky-high ferritin | **Adult-onset Still's** |
