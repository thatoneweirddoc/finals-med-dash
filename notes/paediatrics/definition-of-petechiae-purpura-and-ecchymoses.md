## Definition of petechiae, purpura, and ecchymoses


### Paediatrics (ANU) — Definition of petechiae, purpura, and ecchymoses

- ![](notes/images/crs-5-bruising-anu/img002.jpg)

![](notes/images/crs-5-bruising-anu/img003.jpg)
Purpura- Bleeding under the skin or into mucosal membranes due to disruptions in vascular integrity or abnormalities in primary/secondary hemostasis. They are non-blanching.
- Petechiae are reddish-purple, non-blanchable and non-palpable pinpoint macules (less than 2 mm) of haemorrhage.
- Ecchymoses (AKA bruises) are larger confluent lesions and can be tender or raised.
