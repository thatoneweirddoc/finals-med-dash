## Measles


### Paediatrics (ANU) — Measles

- Measles virus
- Risk factors- immunodeficiency, immunosuppression, corticosteroid therapy, travel to risk places, late winter/spring
- Pathophysiology- Respiratory mucosa 🡪 lymphatics 🡪 blood 🡪 skin and respiratory tract 🡪 coryza and rash
- Transmission- Infectious particles from burst vesicles and respiratory tract infect via the respiratory route. Patient is infective until lesions crusted. Prodromal period 1-2 days most infectious.
- PreventionLive attenuated vaccine (not for immuno-compromised)
- MMR at 12 months, MMRV at 18 months

- SymptomsProdrome- high fever. hacking/brassy cough, exudative conjunctivitis, rhinorrhea, OM, enanthem (Koplik’s spots)
- Exanthem- blotchy, raised, starts behind ears; fever resolves rapidly

- Complications- OM (deafness), pneumonia, croup, measles encephalitis (rare but serious), keratitis of eyes, subacute sclerosing panencephalitis (fatal neurological condition, 7-8 years after measles infection, seizures, coma, death)
- Diagnosis- clinical (including Koplik’s spots), laboratory tests (positive measles IgM)
