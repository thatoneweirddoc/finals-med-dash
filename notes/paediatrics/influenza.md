## Influenza


### Paediatrics (ANU) — Influenza

- ![](notes/images/crs-2-vaccines-and-preventable-diseases-anu/img006.jpg)
RNA virus; Common strains H. Influenza A and B
- Spread- person-person, respiratory secretions, contaminated objects
- PreventionSubunit/conjugate vaccine
- Annual vaccination

- High risk groups- children &lt; 5 years, people with pre-existing disorders e.g. asthma, cystic fibrosis, pregnant/post-partum women, obesity, nursing home/chronic care facilities
- Diagnosis- clinical, laboratory testing if it will affect management
- Management- supportive
- Treatment- antiviral drugs
- Complications- pneumonia, secondary bacterial coinfection (e.g. staph aureus), asthma exacerbation, croup, respiratory failure, toxic shock syndrome, otitis media, CNS (febrile seizures, meningitis, cerebellar ataxia), myositis, pericarditis
