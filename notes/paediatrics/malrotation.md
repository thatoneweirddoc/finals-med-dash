## Malrotation


### Paediatrics (ANU) — Malrotation

- Definition-Intestinal malrotation is a congenital anomaly of rotation of the mid-gut and may result in a volvulus (complete twisting of loop of intestine around its mesenteric attachment site) or acute obstruction.
- HistoryDependant on specific mechanism of obstruction, at any age but most often at &lt;1 month age
- Bilious vomiting
- Concern: Haematochezia, abdominal distension, shock → P/C chronic vomiting, poor weight gain

- Examination Initially normal → May rapidly progression to acute abdomen secondary to bowel necrosis
- High risk of mid-gut volvulus and intestinal necrosis

- InvestigationsAXR → Obstruction (dilatation of stomach/duodenum)
- Upper GI series (corkscrew duodenum, small bowel to the right of midline)

- ManagementSurgical emergency → Evolving ischemic bowel loops
- IVF, NBM, analgesia
- Surgical exploration/Ladd’s procedure: Volvulus untwisted, necrotic segments resected, caecum mobilised, mesenteric base broadened to prevent recurrent volvulus, appendectomy usually done
