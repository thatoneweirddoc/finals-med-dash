## mecnoium aspiration


### Paediatrics (ANU) — mecnoium aspiration

- Hypoxia provokes the baby into passing meconium 🡪inhaled while the baby is gasping for breath during/after birth
- Blocks airways, impedes gas exchange and transmits vasoactive chemicals e.g. endothelin and cytokines to the lungs
- Causes hypoxia, acidosis, pulmonary HTN and complications of pneumothorax
- Management- intubation, aspirating down the ET tube, assist ventilation, surfactant, antibiotics, vasodilators
