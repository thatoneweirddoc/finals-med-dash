## Hypoglycemia


### Paediatrics (ANU) — Hypoglycemia

- Serum glucose concentration &lt; 2.6 mmol/L in babies
- By 2-4 hours,  neonates regulate their own serum glucose
- Hypoglycemia risk factorsExcess fetal insulin levels in diabetic mothers, post-term infants, LGA babies
- Inadequate glycogen stores- premature infants (hepatic immaturity), SGA  babies

- Other causes- excess insulin (absence of urinary ketones), inadequate glucose intake, deficient hyperglycemic hormones (growth and adrenal hormones), fatty acid oxidation/carbohydrate metabolism disorders, sepsis
- Clinical features- altered mental status, poor feeding, high-pitched cry, cyanosis, apnoea, apnea, hypothermia, lethargy
- Diagnosis- Capillary serum glucose check
- Complications-  seizures, serious brain injury
- TreatmentMonitor BGL routines 1-2 hours after birth, until feedings and glucose levels normalized
- IV Bolus (10% dextrose 2mL/kg) and commence IV infusion 10% dextrose (increase infusion by 20 ml/kg/day if BSL remains low)
- Increased fluid
- Try attempt sucking, on formula or on NGT
