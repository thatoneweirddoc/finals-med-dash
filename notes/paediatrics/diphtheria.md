## Diphtheria


### Paediatrics (ANU) — Diphtheria

- Cornibacterium Diptheriae (gram positive)
- Spread- infects respiratory mucous membrane, causing an inflammatory reaction. It produces an endotoxin 🡪 inhibits protein synthesis 🡪 local tissue necrosis. It also forms a pseudomembrane of necrotic film (grey) adheres to underlying tissue with dislodgement causing bleeding
- Presentation- Insidious; sore throat, cervical lymphadenopathy (bull neck), low-grade fever, dysphagia, hoarseness
- Complications- systemic toxicity, myocarditis, neuritis, asphyxiation
- Management- antibiotics (erythromycin, penicillin), antitoxins, airway management, isolation precaustions, serial ECG, neurological status + antimicrobial prophylaxis for close contacts
- Prevention- toxoid vaccine (against toxin produced); given at 2,3,6 months and 4,7 years
