## Psychogenic Seizures


### Paediatrics (ANU) — Psychogenic Seizures

- Non-epileptic seizures (PNES) are events resembling an epileptic seizure, but without the characteristic electrical discharges associated with epilepsy.
- Common age of presentation teenagers
- Often have associated epilepsy
- Likely to be psychogenic seizures when there is absence of injury, bruising, and incontinence, post-ictal stage, respond extremely poorly to anti-convulsants
- Frequently socio-economic stressors
