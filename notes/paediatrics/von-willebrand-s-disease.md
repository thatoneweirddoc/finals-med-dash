## Von Willebrand’s Disease


### Paediatrics (ANU) — Von Willebrand’s Disease

- Pathophysiology
- Autosomal dominant coagulation abnormality
- vWF is needed for platelet adhesion and acts as carrier for Factor VIII. Thus vWF abnormality 🡪 affects hemostasis
- Classification- Type 1 (quantitative defect), Type 2 (activity defect), Type 3 (no vWF produced; rare)
- Clinical
- Mild- asymptomatic, mucosal/cutaneous bleeding, easy bruising, epitaxis, menorrhagia
- ![](notes/images/crs-5-bruising-anu/img006.jpg)
Moderate to Severe- As above, but soft tissue hematomas, petechiae, GI bleeding, hemarthroses
- Investigations- on the right
- Treatment- Desmopressin to cause release of vWF and Factor VIII is treatment of choice
