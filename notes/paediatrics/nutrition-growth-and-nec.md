## nutrition, growth and nec


### Paediatrics (ANU) — nutrition, growth and nec

#### nutrition

- In children born &lt;28 weeks, feeds should be increased very slowly. The target feed is 150 mL/kg/day for babies &gt; 33 weeks.
- Adequacy of nutrition assessed primarily by weight gain- target is ~15g/kg/day
- ![](notes/images/crs-1-newborn-anu/img002.jpg)
What milk? Maternal expressed breast milk (better feed tolerance, reduced NEC/sepsis risk, better neurodevelopmental outcomes); formula milk; fortification/low birth weight formula (can be stopped when weight &gt;2 kg, taking at least 3 suck feeds a day, gaining weight adequately)

#### growth

- Considered preterm if born before 37 weeks
- For babies born &lt;37, first  use preterm growth charts
- Gestational correction adjusts plotting on standard graphs for how early baby was born (40 weeks minus gestational age at birth)
- Gestational correction should be continued for 1 year for 32-37 weeks gestation and 2 years for &lt;32 weeks gestation
- Prematurity- Babies born before 37 weeks; higher risk of disability/deathExtremely preterm (&lt;28 weeks)
- Very preterm (28 – &lt;32 weeks)
- Moderate to late preterm (32 – &lt;37 weeks)

#### necorotising enterocolitis

- Prematurity and Low birth weight are the two greatest risk factors for NEC
- Portions of the bowel undergo necrosis
- PathogenesisAetiology not fully known; potential genetic factor, doesn’t occur in utero
- Suggested that there the main issue is through damage to the bowel mucosa
- Potential colonisation of mucosa with pathogenic bacteria might delay development of innate immune system

- Complications- Perforation, Sepsis, Abscess formation, Strictures, Short bowel syndrome (after surgical intervention)
- Clinical Features- Abdominal distension, Bloody stools + mucous, Bilious vomit, Decreased bowel sounds
- Diagnosis- ABDOMINAL XRAY!!, Blood tests not specific (FBC, gas, biochem, CRP)
- Staging - Stage 1 – Suspected NEC; Stage 2 – Definitely but mild/moderate; Stage 3 – Advanced +/- Perf

- Management- NBM, NG Tube, IVF, TPN, IV ABx
