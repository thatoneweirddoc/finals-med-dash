## Polio


### Paediatrics (ANU) — Polio

- Enterovirus (C group)
- Eradicated from Australia since 2000
- Transmission- faecal-hand-oral contamination
- Risk factors- overcrowding, age &lt; 5, non-vaccinated, tonsillectomy
- 90-95% asymptomatic/low-grade viral illness however it may progress and involve the CNS, causing the selective destruction of motor neurons (back/neck/muscle pain, motor weakness)
- Complications- paralysis (spinal/bulbar/bulbospinal) causing paralysis of arms/legs and/or swallowing/speech difficulties, septic arthritis, meningitis
- Diagnosis- clinical presentation, PCR amplification of poliovirus RNA in CSF (gold standard)
- Treatment- no cure, symptomatic, anti-viral in acute flaccid paralysis, long-term rehabitilation/physiotherapy, orthopedia surgery
- PreventionInactivated vaccine
- Doses given at 2,4, and 6 months of age with a booster dose at 4 years of age
