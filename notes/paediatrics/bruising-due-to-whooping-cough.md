## Bruising due to whooping cough


### Paediatrics (ANU) — Bruising due to whooping cough

- Refer to CRS 2 for Whooping Cough details
- PathogenesisCoughing 🡪 increased friction between intercostal muscles in rib cages 🡪 minor injuries 🡪 blood vessels “burst” 🡪 small clots
- Post-tussive vomiting 🡪 bruising in thoracic region
- Rise in intrathoracic pressure 🡪 small bruises on other parts of the body

- Complication- bruising your ribs 🡪 pneumonia due to stagnant lung tissue as a result of altered breathing mechanisms from fractures and bruises
