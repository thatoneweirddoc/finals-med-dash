## Possible Exam Questions


### Paediatrics (ANU) — Possible Exam Questions

**What are the contraindications to immunization?**

- Absolute:Acute febrile illness &gt;38.5
- Previous severe reaction
- Previous vaccines within 1/12

- Relative: Immunocompromised
- No live vaccines during pregnancy

**What’s the difference between passive and active immunization?**

- Active: your body makes immunoglobulin against the antibody administered
- Passive: you are given preformed immunoglobulin (from a blood donor)

**How many immunisations are given at 4 months?**

- 2 needles and 1 oral
- Infanrix (hepB-DTPa-Hib-IPV)
- Pneumococcal
- Rotavirus (oral)

**Which bacteria cause meningitis in an unimmunized population?**

- HiB, pneumococcal, meningococcus.
- Give an example of a live vaccine Measles, mumps, rubella
- Rotavirus
- Varicella

**Give an example of a killed vaccine **

- Polio (IPV= inactivated polio vaccine)
- Give an example of a toxoid vaccine
- Tetanus, diphtheria

**List 4 complications of measles **

- Pnuemonia
- Encephalitis
- SSPE
- Keratitits

**List 4 complications of chickenpox **

- Pneumonia
- Encephalitis
- Skin abscess
- Distal infection e.g. osteomyelitis
- Congenital varicella.

**How would you approach a parent of an unimmunized child at 18 months?**

- Ask why: find out reasons for not vaccinating e.g. belief systems, disrupted family.
- Explain reasons for vaccine
- Facilitate immunization
- Show respect

**If they agree to immunization, how would you proceed?**

Catch-up course – vaccines need to be 1 month apart.

E.g. in an 18/12 old child who has only received 2 and 4 month vaccines:

- Give 6mth immunisations now
- Give 12mth immunisations in 1 month
- Give 18mth immunisations in 2mth (from now)

Can ring Australian childhood immunization register to see what they’ve had and arrange catch up.
