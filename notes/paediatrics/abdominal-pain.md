## Abdominal Pain


### Paediatrics (ANU) — Abdominal Pain

#### Key learning outcomes

- Issues to consider when assessing children with abdominal pain
- Signs and symptoms of serious illness
- When to get senior medical review
- Options for pain relief
- Discharging criteria for abdominal pain
