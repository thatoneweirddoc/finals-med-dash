## Other causes of respiratory distress


### Paediatrics (ANU) — Other causes of respiratory distress

- Viral/bacterial infections e.g. coup, common could influenza, bronchiolitis
- Allergies
- Exposure to cigarette smoke- impair lung development
- Blockage- foreign body e.g. food/small toy
- Genetic causes- cystic fibrosis
- Transient tachypnea of the newborn
- Infant respiratory distress syndrome- previously, HMD
- Meconium aspiration
- Infection
- Pneumothorax
- Persistent pulmonary hypertension- occurs when pulmonary vascular resistance fails to decrease soon after birth. It can be idiopathic or secondary to meconium aspiration syndrome, pneumonia or sepsis
- Congenital malformations e.g. pulmonary hypoplasia, upper airway obstruction, obstructive lesions e.g. choanal atresia, congenital heart disease
- Neurological disorders- hydrocephalus, intracranial haemorrhage
- Metabolic/haematological derangements- hypoglycaemia, hypoCa2+, polycythemia, anaemia
- Choonal atresia- congenital, choana (back of nasal passage) blocked by abnormal bony/soft tissue due to failed recanalization of nasal fossae
- Pulmonary hypolasia- incomplete lung development 🡪 ↓bronchopulmonary segments/alveoli
