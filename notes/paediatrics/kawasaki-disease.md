## KAWASAKI DISEASE


### Paediatrics (ANU) — KAWASAKI DISEASE

- Diagnostic criteriaFever for more than 5 days often over 39 degrees and not responsive to antibiotics
- AND 4/5 of the following:Polymorphous rash in first 5 days ; often more in groin region, macular popular
- Bilateral (non-purulent) conjunctivitis
- Cervical lymphadenopathy &gt;1.5cm diameter, unilateral, painful, non-purulent
- Mucous membrane changes – red/dry/cracked lips, strawberry tongue, diffuse redress of oral or pharyngeal mucosa
- Peripheral changes- Acute (Red/swollen hands/feet, palm/sole erythema); subacute (periungual peeling of fingers and toes)

- PathophysiologyT4 hypersensitivity reaction 🡪 effect on all arteries especially medium sized arteries – stiffens them
- Biggest cause of acquired heart disease in children in 1st world countries

- DDx- Stevens-Johnson, Toxic Shock Syndrome
