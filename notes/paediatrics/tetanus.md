## Tetanus


### Paediatrics (ANU) — Tetanus

- Clostridium Tetani
- SpreadBacteria releases endospores which germinate anaerobic environments (e.g ischemic wounds) 🡪 inhibit GABA release at motor neurone 🡪 loss of inhibition of motor/autonomic neurons
- Infects scratches and broken skin from soil, dust or manure

- Risk Factors- IVDU, older adults
- Signs- painful muscle spasms that begin in jaw (lock jaw), stiff neck/shoulder/back, dysphagia, convulsions, breathing difficulties, nerve/muscle changes 🡪 paralysis
- Complications- respiratory failure, cardiac arrest, broken bones from spasms
- Treatment- tetanus immunoglobulin, metronidazole IV, diazepam, antibiotics (penicillin)
- Prevention- Toxoid vaccine at 2,4,6 months of age, booster at 4 years, secondary school booster
