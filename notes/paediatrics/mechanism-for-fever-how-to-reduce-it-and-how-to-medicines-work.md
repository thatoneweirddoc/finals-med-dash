## Mechanism for fever; how to reduce it and how to medicines work


### Paediatrics (ANU) — Mechanism for fever; how to reduce it and how to medicines work

- Definition- Fever by guidelines definition greater than or equal to 38 degrees taken from axillary
- Upper limit ~42oC
- Pathogenesis Peripheral synthesis of IL-1, IL-6, TNF, IFN-α, other endogenous pyrogenic cytokines by phagocytic cells in blood/tissues
- Cytokines enter blood → carried to anterior hypothalamus → induce abrupt ↑ in prostaglandin synthesis (esp. E2) → raises hypothalamic set-point for body temperature
- Thermoregulatory center now recognizes body temp to be too ↓ → initiates methods of raising body temperature to new set point, including;↑ metabolic rate, muscle tone and activity → ↑heat production
- Diminished perfusion through skin → ↓ heat loss

- Temperature rises until new equilibrium is achieved
- Mechanisms of Reducing Fever 1. AntipyreticsParacetamol (15mg/kg/dose upto 4 hours, max dose 4 doses/24 hours). inhibition of the hypothalamic heat-regulating center (proposed MOA)
- Ibuprofen (not recommended &lt;6 months; 10mg/kg/dose upto 6 hourly, max dose 4 doses/24hours). Inhibits COX1/COX2 → ↓ formation of prostaglandin precursors → antipyretic/analgesic/anti-inflammatory
- Alternating not recommended
- Note: Aspirin could lead to Reye Syndrome (encephalopathy with hepatic dysfunction); thus avoided

- 2. Physical Methods- unwrapping overdressed child
