## juvenile arthritis- polyarticular, oligoarticular, and systemic


### Paediatrics (ANU) — juvenile arthritis- polyarticular, oligoarticular, and systemic

- Classification- three subsets based on number of joints involved
- Polyarticular - rash, intermittent fever, arthritis of any number of joints
- Oligoarticular - &lt;5 joints after 6 months of illness
- Systemic- &gt; 4 joints after 6 months of illness
- Pathogenesis- unclear, seems to be autoimmune (MHC)
- Symptoms- fever, arthritis, rash (macular, salmon pink), lymphadenopathy
- Clinical Findings- Exam, Hepatosplenomegaly, Lymphadenopathy
- Labs- ↑WCC, Anaemia, ↑ CRP, ↑ ferritin
- Diagnosis- Diagnosis of exclusion (exclude malignancy, autoimmune, joint infection), fevers &gt; 2 weeks, arthritis &gt; 6 weeks
- Treatment- NSAIDs, biological DMARDs, glucocorticoids, paediatric rheumatologist referral
