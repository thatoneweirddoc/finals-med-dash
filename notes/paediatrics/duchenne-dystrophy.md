## Duchenne dystrophy


### Paediatrics (ANU) — Duchenne dystrophy

- X-linked disorder, occurring exclusively in males
- Aetiology- mutation on chromosome Xp21, causing loss of dystrophin which normally stabilises the contractile apparatus within muscle fibres. Hereditary risk factor
- Outcome- death from respiratory/cardiac failure in second decade of life
- Symptoms- usually recognised from 18 monthsLate walking &gt;18 months
- Gower’s manoeuvre- due to proximal muscle weakness
- Enlargement (pseudohypertrophy) of calf, quadriceps and triceps
- Intellectual impairment, delayed language development, ↓IQ
- Proximal muscle weakness increasing over time 🡪 waddling gait, toe-walking, lumbar lordosis, increasing talipes foot deformities 🡪wheelchair b/w 8-13 years
- Second decade of life🡪 decline in pulmonary function (relating to scoliosis, progressive muscle weakness)

- Diagnosis- FHx, clinical features (gait), serum CK, DNA testing, muscle biopsy
- Genetic counselling in known FHx, males not working before 18 months, unexplained gait disturbance, unexplained mental retardation, unexplained language delay
- Treatment- no cure; corticosteroid improves muscle strength, prolongs independent ambulation and preserves respiratory/cardiac function; ACEi slows cardiomyopathy; nocturnal ventilator support
