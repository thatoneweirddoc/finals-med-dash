## Fevers lasting more than a week (7+ days)


### Paediatrics (ANU) — Fevers lasting more than a week (7+ days)

- Still most commonly infectious origin
- Must consider- Connective tissue disorders, Malignancy

**Infectious causes**

- LocalisedOM
- Meningitis/encephalitis
- Periorbital cellulitis
- Sinusitis/tonsillitis
- URTI/LRTI
- Gastroenteritis
- Appendicitis
- Hepatitis
- UTI
- Joints – septic arthritis

- Generalised- Septicaemia
- Recent exposures? Animals, travel, contacts, immunisations

**Non infectious causes**

- CT diseasesJuvenile rheumatoid arthritis
- SLE
- Dermatomyositis
- Scleroderma
- Sarcoidosis
- Vasculitis – Wegener granulomatosis, polyarteritis nodosum

- Inflammatory- IBD, Kawasaki
- Malignancy- Leukaemia, Lymphoma
- Drug reaction- Antibiotics (penicillin), Phenytoin
- Heat exposure
- Metabolic diseases
- Teething- NB teething wont cause fever over 38.4

#### Management = full septic work up

- Indicated in child &lt;3 months with rectal temp &gt; 38 or child &gt;3 months with fever of unknown focus with temp over 38
- Septic work upBLOODSFBC (WCC)
- Coags – disseminated intravascular coagulopathy, hypercoagualability
- EUC, LFT: disseminated viral and anaerobic infections
- CRP/ESR: inflammatory
- Culture: before start antibiotics

- URINE- Clean catch/ in-out catheter/ suprapubic tapCulture For toxic and &lt; 3 months, before antibiotics
- Most common = E.Coli, proteus, klebsiella, E faecalis

- DipstickNon-toxic, &gt;3 months
- Haem 🡪 glomerula, trauma, UTI, obstruction
- Nitrites 🡪 produced by bacteria
- Leukocyte esterase 🡪 neutrophils/UTI

- LUMBAR PUNCTUREIndication: young, toxic, febrile convulsions
- IV fluids, resus, ABx BEFORE LP
- Contraindications to LP = ICP, local infection at site, seizing, unstable

- CHEST XRAY- If respiratory signs and symptoms or no focus of infection found

#### On Examination 🡪 NB the following findings

- Any signs of increased WOB 🡪 PNEUMONIA
- Stiff neck or bulging fontanelle 🡪 MENINGITIS
- Red tender joint 🡪 SEPTIC ARTHRITIS/ RHEUMATIC FEVER
- Petechial or purpuric rash 🡪 MENINGOCOCCAL/ DENGUE
- Maculopapular rash 🡪 VIRAL/ DRUG RXN
- Inflamed throat and mucous membranes 🡪 SINUSITIS/TONSILITIS
- Red, painful ear with immobile ear-drum 🡪 OTITIS MEDIA
- Jaundice or anaemia) 🡪 MALARIA, HEPATITIS, LEPTOSPIROSIS, SEPTICAEMIA
- Painful spine, hips or other joints 🡪 SEPTIC ARTHRITIS
- Tender abdomen (suprapubic or loin in urinary tract infection) 🡪 UTI
- No localising signs 🡪 SEPTICAEMIA, SALMONELLA, MILITARY TB, HIV, UTI
