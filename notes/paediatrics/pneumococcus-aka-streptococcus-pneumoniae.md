## pneumococcus (aka streptococcus pneumoniae)


### Paediatrics (ANU) — pneumococcus (aka streptococcus pneumoniae)

- Gram positive; encapsulated
- Transmission- respiratory droplets
- Spread- colonise nasopharyngeal epithelial cells

![](notes/images/crs-2-vaccines-and-preventable-diseases-anu/img007.jpg)

- Risk groups- &lt;2 years of age, immunocompromised, &gt;65 years, chronic disease e.g. diabetes
- Symptoms- depends on body part affected; symptoms of meningitis, pneumonia, depending on body part affected
- Complications- hearing loss, brain damage, death, osteomyelitis, septic arthritis
- Diagnosis- physical exam, CSR, FBC and blood cultures, lumbar puncture
- PreventionNo vaccine protects against all 90 straigns of pneumonia
- Conjugate vaccine PCV 13- Given at 2,4 and 6 months
