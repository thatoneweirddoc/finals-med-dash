## Duodenal atresia


### Paediatrics (ANU) — Duodenal atresia

- Definition: Intestinal atresia is a malformation where there is narrowing/absence of a portion of the small/large bowel. The most common cause is a vascular accident in utero → Decreased intestinal perfusion → Ischaemia
- History: Polyhydramnios (foetal), symptoms appear soon after birth, feeding intolerance, bilious vomiting
- Examination Abdominal distension (absent in proximal atresia, severe with visible loops in distal)
- Tenderness (peritonitis), failure to pass meconium, signs of volume depletion

- InvestigationsFoetal: US (dilated intestinal segment), polyhydramnios
- Plain AXR: Double bubble sign, proximal presence of gas with distal absence (→ upper GI series)

- Management: Urgent surgical correction of the small intestinal atresia
