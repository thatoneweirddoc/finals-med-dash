## ENCEPHALITIS


### Paediatrics (ANU) — ENCEPHALITIS

- Syndrome of neurological dysfunction caused by inflammation of the brain parenchyma
- Causes: Viruses (HSV and enterovirus most common), direct infection by small intracellular bacteria, mycoplasma pneumonia, syphilis, some parasites, malaria
- Signs and symptomsWaxing and waning fever
- Altered state of consciousness, headache
- Focal neurological signs
- Prior seizures and subtle motor seizures
- Photophobia, papilloedema, neck stiffness
- Flaccid paralysis, movement disorders (including Parkinsonism)
- Rash: Purpuric (meningococcal), vesicular (hand, foot and mouth disease, varicella zoster)

- Diagnosis: PCR, inflammatory changes in CSF, parenchymal inflammation on imaging
- Treatment → Viral: AcyclovirIV fluids: Na+ and 2/3 maintenance → SIADH is common in CNS infections

- MonitoringCNS complications 🡪 Development, hearing
- In Meningococcus, antibiotic use may 🡪 Bacteriolysis/inflammatory cascade → Sick
