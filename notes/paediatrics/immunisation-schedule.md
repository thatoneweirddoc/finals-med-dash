## immunisation schedule


### Paediatrics (ANU) — immunisation schedule

- Birth: Hep B
- 2, 4 and 6 Months: Infanrix (hepB-DTPa-Hib-IPV = Diphtheria, Tetanus, Acellular Pertussis, HiB, Polio (IPV=inactivated Polio vax)), Pneumococcal, Rotavirus
- 12 months: HiB and meningococcal (Hib-MenC), MMR
- 18 Months: MMR
- 4 years: DTPa-IPV, MMR if not at 18/12
- 10-15 years: Varicella, HPV, Boostrix (Diptheria, tetanus, acellular pertussis)
