## What bruises are normal?


### Paediatrics (ANU) — What bruises are normal?

- *Refer to Child Protection Lecture
- Normal bruise: Undergo colour changes from red to purple, to green to yellow-brown colour before disappearing completely, over 2-3 weeks
- Walking children: Common in bony regions of the body → Head, forehead (falls), shins, elbows, shoulders, hips
- Abnormal or uncommon bruising may include: Large, raised, out of proportion for the injury that caused it.
- ![](notes/images/crs-5-bruising-anu/img004.jpg)
Unexplained bruises without any history of a fall or injury
- Bruises that seem to last too long (more than a few weeks).
- In infants who have not started to crawl, cruise, or walk yet
- In soft unusual places →  chest, back, hands, ear, face or buttocks
- FMHx of easy bruising/bleeding disorders (many are genetic)
- Epistaxis lasting &gt; 15 mins after proper treatment and direct pressure
- Excess bleeding after dental procedures/surgery
