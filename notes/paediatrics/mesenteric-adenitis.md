## mesenteric adenitis


### Paediatrics (ANU) — mesenteric adenitis

- Inflammation of the mesenteric lymph nodes and usually presents in children with a recent history of URTI
- Aetiologies- Viral and bacterial gastroenteritis, GAS pharyngitis, IBD, Lymphoma, Viral infection
- Signs and symptomsThe nodes are usually in the right lower quadrant 🡪 RIF pain
- Pain in the abdomen is usually diffuse with tenderness not localised to the right lower quadrant
- Guarding may be present but rigidity is usually absent
- Fever, ?Diarrhoea, N/V, malaise

- InvestigationsImaging to differentiate from appendicitis (US or CT)
- U/S – shows abdominal lymph nodes greater than 10mm

- Management – generally goes away on its own, pain management
