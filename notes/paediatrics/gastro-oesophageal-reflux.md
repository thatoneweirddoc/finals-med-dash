## Gastro-oesophageal reflux


### Paediatrics (ANU) — Gastro-oesophageal reflux

- Definition: Retrograde movement of gastric contents into oesophagus secondary to transient lower oesophageal relaxation
- PathophysiologyPhysiological- incomplete competency of LOS (6 weeks-12 months of age)
- Pathological- Anatomical (more frequent/persistent), structural (hiatus hernia), neurological (cerebral palsy 🡪 oesophagitis, pulmonary aspiration, Barret’s, failure to thrive

- Presentation- regurgitation (post-prandial), irritability with arching, substernal/epigastric pain, dysphagia/odynophagia, irritability, weight loss (red flag) and hematemesis (red flag), FTT, cough/wheeze/apnea
- Investigation- oesophageal PH monitoring (gold standard), oesphagoxcopy +/- biopsy
- Treatment- physiological resolves spontaneously, thickened fluids, pharmacological (H2 receptor antagonist, PPI preferred in infants- &lt;10kg-5mg/day, 10-20kg max 10mg/day, &gt;20 kg 20mg/day)
