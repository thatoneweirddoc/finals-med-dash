## Intususseption


### Paediatrics (ANU) — Intususseption

- Definition- Invagination of a proximal segment of bowel into the distal bowel lumen commonly ileo-colic can also be colic-colic and ileal-ileal
- HistoryIntermittent pain: Colicky, severe, drawing up the legs; episodes 2-3 times/hour
- Associated-: Pallor, lethargy, vomiting (bile late sign), blood/mucus stools (red currant jelly), diarrhoea
- May be a preceding respiratory or diarrhoeal illness

- Examination Pallor, lethargy: May be intermittent and look well in between
- Abdominal mass: Sausage shaped mass RUQ or crossing midline in epigastrium/behind umbilicus, palpable in about 2/3
- Late: Distended abdomen, hypovolaemic shock
- PR if uncertain for signs of PR bleed/mass, stool (bloody stool/occult blood positive)

- Investigations
- Bloods: FBC, UEC, BSL, Group and hold if theatre
- Plain abdominal x-ray- Target sign (2 concentric circular radiolucent lines RUQ), Crescent sign (crescent shaped lucency in LUQ)
- Ultrasound scan: Diagnostic investigation of choice (unnecessary if high level of suspicion)
- Air enema: Diagnostic and therapeutic, initial investigation of choice if strong clinical suspicion (Small risk of bowel perforation and bacteraemia)
- Management- IVC, IVF, analgesia, NBM, NGT if bowel obstruction/perforation, consider IV Abx before air enema
