## Haemophilus influenzae type b


### Paediatrics (ANU) — Haemophilus influenzae type b

- Gram negative coccobacillus
- Pathophysiology- polysaccharide capsule which cant be phagocytosed evade the immune system, producing neuramidase and IgA protease and fimbriae adhere to respiratory mucosa ciliary cells
- Transmission- direct contact, respiratory droplets
- Symptoms- symptoms of an URTI which may progress to LRTI (resembling wheezy bronchitis, grey/creamy sputum colour)
- Complications- meningitis, septic arthritis, pericarditis, pleuritic, epiglottis
- Diagnosis- bacterial culture
- Treatment- beta-lactams (amoxicillin or 2nd/3rd generation cephalosporin) usually
- PreventionSubunit/conjugate
- Doses given at 2,4, and 6 months of age with a booster at 12 months
