## learning objectives


### Paediatrics (ANU) — learning objectives

- Mesenteric Adenitis
- Appendicitis
- Gastro-oesophageal reflux
- Pyloric Stenosis +/- electrolyte changes
- CNS Causes of vomiting e.g. concussion, raised ICP, tumour
- Hirchsprung’s Disease and Chronic Constipation
- Testicular Torsion
- Non-surgical causes of abdominal pain including pneumonia, urinary tract infection, diabetic ketoacidosis, abdominal migraine
- Duodenal atresia, Malrotation, intususseption
- What is the difference between possetting, projectile and reflux vomiting, and why do we worry about bilious vomiting
- CPG Abdominal Pain *refer to CPG notes
