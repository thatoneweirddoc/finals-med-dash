## Rota Infections


### Paediatrics (ANU) — Rota Infections

- 7 major groups of rotavirus A to G (A=human illness)
- Spread- Infect and destroy mature enterocytes in villous epithelium of proximal small intestine 🡪 loss of absorptive epithelium and proliferation of secretory crypt cells 🡪 secretory diarrhea
- Transmission- Fecal oral
- Symptoms- Range from subclinical to severe gastroenteritis 🡪 life threatening dehydration; abrupt onset, vomiting then diarrhea, low grade fever, right, olive green, loose and watery stools. GIT symptoms resolve in 3-7 days
- Complications- Hyper/hypo2+ with dehydration, metabolic acidosis from bicarbonate loss in stool, ketosis from poor oral intake, hypotension and hypoperfusion (lactic acidemia)
- PreventionLive attenuated virus vaccine; catch-up doses cannot be given
- Given at 2+4 months or 2+4+6 months depending on vaccine brand used
