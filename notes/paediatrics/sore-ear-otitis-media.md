## Sore Ear- Otitis Media


### Paediatrics (ANU) — Sore Ear- Otitis Media

#### KEY LEARNING OUTCOMES

- Definitions relating to presentations of sore ears
- Possible causes
- Management according to age
- D/C Criteria

#### Definitions

- Many VRTI are accompanies with inflammation of the middle ear, visible redness, dull tympanic membrane

| CONDITION | OVERVIEW |
| --- | --- |
| ACUTE OTITIS MEDIA | Definition: Inflammation and purulent discharge in the middle ear, accompanies with bulging, inflamed TM which has decreased motilityAssessment: pain, fever, irritability, otoscopy of bulging inflamed TMCulture not required, Can be bacterial infectionManagement&lt;2 years- Analgesia, 24 hour reevaluation&gt;2 years- Analgesia 48 hours, consider Abx therapy if no response |
| MYRINGITIS (RED EARDRUM) | Definition: Inflammation of the TM alone in association with otitis externalAssessment: mild redness of eardrumAntibiotics not neededManagementAnalgesia 48 hours, consider Abx therapy if no response |
| OTITIS MEDIA WITH EFFUSION (OME) | Definition: Serous otitis media with fluid in middle ear without symptoms of acute inflammation of the ear. Most are residual effusions that remain after AOM treatment, and may last 6-15 weeksAntibiotics not neededManagementAcute- Analgesia 48 hours, consider Abx therapy if no responseChronic- ENT referral |
| UNRESPONSIVE AOM | Definition: Otoscopic findings of inflammation of the eardrums that continue beyond 48 hours of therapy |
| RECURRENT AOM (ROAM) | Definition: Three episodes of AOM within a six-month period |
| CHRONIC SUPPURATIVE OM | Definition: Persistent inflammatory process associated with a perforated TM and draining exudate for more than six weeks |
| OTITIS EXTERNA | Definition: Inflammation of the outer ear canal, often due to bacterial/fungal/yeast. It can occue in isolation of associated with AOM and perforation of the TM |
| MASTOIDITIS | Definition: Osteomyelitis of the mastoid bone and a complication of AOM. Characterised by post-auricular swelling and redness |

#### diagnosis and treatment

- Pain relief- paracetamol/ibuprofen
- Auralgan Otic Solution- Combination product of antipyrine, benzocaine and glycerine provides variable relief within 30mins
- Antibiotic Therapy (don’t want to anti-microbial resistance development)Consider in chronic OME (&gt;3 months) with hearing loss or drum retraction
- AOM in 6 months-2 years- symptomatic treatment, consider Abx
- AOM in &gt;2 years- most cases resolve alone and there is no need for Abx

- Follow-Up- Especially if patient is symptomatic at 48-72 hours following symptomatic analgesic treatment. Reassess for complications, other diagnoses and compliance with medications
- Referral- OME&gt;3 months + hearing loss, 3 or more episodes of AOM in 6 months, 4 or more episodes of AOM in 12 months, retracted TM
