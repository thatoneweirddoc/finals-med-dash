## Cardiomyopathies


### Cardiology Notes — Cardiomyopathies

##### 7.1 Dilated Cardiomyopathy (DCM)
Dilated, poorly contracting ventricles (systolic dysfunction). **Causes:** idiopathic/genetic, **alcohol**, peripartum, post-myocarditis, tachycardia-induced, drugs (**anthracyclines**, trastuzumab), haemochromatosis, thiamine deficiency. Presents as **HFrEF**, arrhythmia, or thromboembolism. Treat as heart failure (Installment 2).

##### 7.2 Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy (HCM)
- **Genetics:** **autosomal dominant** sarcomere-gene mutations (commonly β-myosin heavy chain, myosin-binding protein C).
- **Pathophysiology:** asymmetric septal hypertrophy → **LV outflow tract obstruction** (in HOCM) and diastolic dysfunction; risk of **sudden cardiac death**.
- **Signs:** ejection-systolic murmur that gets **louder with Valsalva/standing** (reduced preload), jerky pulse, double apical impulse, S4.
- **SCD risk factors** (feed the ESC **HCM Risk-SCD** model): family history of SCD, **unexplained syncope**, **NSVT**, **massive LVH (≥30 mm)**, abnormal BP response to exercise; the model also weights age, LA diameter and LVOT gradient. (AHA/ACC additionally flag LVEF <50%, apical aneurysm, extensive LGE.)
- **Management:** **beta-blockers or verapamil** (avoid in significant obstruction with caution); **avoid dehydration, vasodilators (nitrates, ACEi) and digoxin** in obstructive disease; **ICD** if high SCD risk; septal reduction (myectomy or alcohol septal ablation) for refractory obstruction; **mavacamten** (cardiac myosin inhibitor) is a newer option. **Commonest cause of SCD in young athletes.**

##### 7.3 Restrictive Cardiomyopathy
Stiff, non-compliant ventricles → impaired filling with preserved (early) systolic function. **Causes:** **amyloidosis**, sarcoidosis, haemochromatosis, endomyocardial fibrosis. Overlaps clinically with constrictive pericarditis.

##### 7.4 Arrhythmogenic (Right Ventricular) Cardiomyopathy (ARVC)
Fibrofatty replacement of RV myocardium → ventricular arrhythmias and SCD, often in young adults/athletes. ECG clues: **epsilon waves**, T-wave inversion V1–V3.

##### 7.5 Other
- **Takotsubo (stress) cardiomyopathy** — apical ballooning post-stress, unobstructed arteries (Installment 1).
- **Peripartum cardiomyopathy** — HFrEF in late pregnancy to ~5 months postpartum.
- **Cardiac amyloidosis** — increasingly recognised: suspect with **HFpEF + LVH on echo but low-voltage ECG**, bilateral carpal tunnel, aortic stenosis in the elderly; diagnose with bone scintigraphy (DPD/PYP) ± biopsy. **Sarcoidosis** — heart block and VT in a young/middle-aged patient. **Haemochromatosis** — dilated/restrictive picture plus diabetes, bronze skin, arthropathy.

##### C. Disease Formulation Cards

> **Aortic stenosis** — degenerative/bicuspid/rheumatic. Syncope/Angina/Dyspnoea; ESM → carotids, slow-rising pulse, soft S2. Severe: area <1 cm², gradient >40, velocity >4 m/s. Intervene if symptomatic or EF <50% (SAVR/TAVI). *Buzz:* "SAD".

> **Mitral stenosis** — rheumatic. Mid-diastolic rumble, loud S1, opening snap, malar flush, AF. Ix: echo. Mx: balloon valvuloplasty/surgery, anticoagulate if AF. *Buzz:* malar flush + tapping apex.

> **Infective endocarditis** — S. aureus (acute/IVDU) or S. viridans (subacute/teeth). Fever + new murmur + emboli + peripheral stigmata. Ix: 3 blood cultures + TTE→TOE; Duke criteria. Mx: 4–6 wk IV antibiotics ± surgery. *Buzz:* FROM JANE.

> **Acute pericarditis** — viral/idiopathic. Sharp pleuritic pain, worse flat/better forward, friction rub. ECG: widespread saddle ST elevation + PR depression. Mx: NSAIDs + colchicine. *Buzz:* "sitting forward".

> **Cardiac tamponade** — effusion compressing the heart. Beck's triad, pulsus paradoxus, electrical alternans. Ix: echo (diastolic RV collapse). Mx: pericardiocentesis. *Buzz:* "water-bottle heart".

> **HCM** — AD sarcomere mutation, septal hypertrophy ± LVOT obstruction. Murmur louder on Valsalva; young athlete SCD. Mx: β-blocker/verapamil, avoid vasodilators/dehydration, ICD if high risk, mavacamten. *Buzz:* commonest SCD cause in young athletes.

> **Dilated cardiomyopathy** — dilated, poorly contracting ventricles. Alcohol/anthracyclines/peripartum/genetic. Presents as HFrEF ± arrhythmia/emboli. Mx: HF therapy. *Buzz:* "big baggy heart".
