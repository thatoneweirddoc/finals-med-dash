### Constipation & Aperients Summary — Key Differentials Quick-Reference (a view of the Phase-4 recognition summary — not authored separately)

**The clinical definition of constipation**

any two of the following symptoms for at least 12 weeks* in the previous 12 months.

- straining during bowel motions
- lumpy or hard stool
- sensation of incomplete evacuation (tenesmus)
- sensation of anorectal blockage / obstruction
- fewer than three bowel movements per week
- need to use manual things to facilitate poop

* The 12 weeks do not need to be consecutive.

**Investigation**

DRE - blood/impaction of stool; anal sphincter tone, prostate

Bimanual and vaginal examination in women - prolapse, rectocoele, retroverted uterus

Labs - hypothyroidism, hypocalcaemia

Warnings and why

- liquid paraffin (agarol) a stool softener should not be given to kids with CP or GORD
- bran is not good for infants and toddlers 🡪 interferes with absorption of iron and zinc
- glycerine is a good osmotic, but is ABSORBED into the small bowel so can’t use it too much
- castor oil can trigger premature uterine contractions

How do stool softeners work?

- stool softener is an umbrella term -> include surfactant, lubricant, osmotics

- SURFACTANTS are EMULSIFIERS - facilitate admixture of fat and water in faeces, allow water to enter the poop by ↓ surface tension of the stool
- LUBRICANTS delay absorption of water from stools 🡪 make them softer
- OSMOTICS actively draw water into the colon to hydrate dry stools eg lactulose

How do stimulants work? eg senna, bisacodyl

- increase muscle contractions mediated by an enteric reflex
- take a few hours to work
- alter electrolyte transport in intestinal mucosa
- cons - abdo discomfort and cramps

How do the sugary things eg lactulose work as osmotic agents?

- lactulose is disaccharide with galactose and fructose
- we don’t have frustosidase  🡪 can’t digest them
- sit in bowel unabsorbed 🡪 substrate for bacteria to ferment
- produce CO2, water, acid, fatty acids, hydrogen, methane
- cons bloating discomfort flatulence
- pros osmotic AND stimulate intestinal motility and secretions

How do bulking agents work?

- most are polysaccharides or cellulose derivatives
- we can’t digest them 🡪 sit in the colonic lumen and absorb water + increase faecal mass which should stimulate motility and reduce colon transit time (unless pt has neuromuscular pathology in which case you’ll make it worse)
- cons - bran esp can cause abdo bloating and discomfort
- pros - good first line. Want to increase fibre intake to 20-35g daily

How does mineral oil aka poo lube work?

- literally no chemical effect
- coats rectum and lube for your poo
- cons - anal seepage, FB reaction in tissue, lipid pneumonia if you aspirate it, malabsorption of fat soluble vitamins for a while

Which are the fancy aperients that have multiple MOA?

- polyethylene glycol, the thing in Movicol, has water retainer and osmotic agent - draws in water AND keeps it there
- bisacodyl and glycerine suppository - given together - bisacodyl is a stimulant, glycerine softens poo in the anus

Bowel Prep?

- last last last line
- if you don’t want to do a manual disimpaction
- commonly used are picolax/microlax and glycoprep

Picolax

- sodium picosulphate
- 2-3 sachets over 4 hours
- drink LOTS of water
- SFX - dehydration, dizziness, N+V, abdo pain
- use in patients who can withstand fluid and electrolyte shifts, otherwise use glycoprep
