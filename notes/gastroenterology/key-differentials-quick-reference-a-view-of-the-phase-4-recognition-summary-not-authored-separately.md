#### Suggested Sequencing

| Phase | Focus |
|---|---|

#### High-Yield Priorities (if time is short)

1. **Upper GI bleed** — resuscitation, Glasgow–Blatchford, variceal vs non-variceal management (terlipressin + antibiotics + endoscopy)
2. **Decompensated cirrhosis** — the complications set (ascites/SBP, encephalopathy, varices, HRS) and how they're managed
3. **Paracetamol overdose** — nomogram, N-acetylcysteine, King's College criteria
4. **LFT interpretation** — hepatocellular vs cholestatic, AST:ALT, synthetic function
5. **Bowel obstruction & the acute abdomen** — obstruction vs perforation vs ischaemia, when to operate
6. **Inflammatory bowel disease** — Crohn's vs UC discriminators, acute severe UC management
7. **Acute pancreatitis** — diagnosis, Glasgow-Imrie severity, fluids
8. **Gallstone disease spectrum** — biliary colic vs cholecystitis vs cholangitis (Charcot's triad)
9. **Coeliac disease** — anti-tTG, the no-biopsy pathway, associations
10. **Colorectal cancer** — FIT screening, red flags/2-week-wait, staging, Lynch/FAP
