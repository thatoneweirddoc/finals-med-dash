## Interstitial (Diffuse Parenchymal) Lung Disease


### Respiratory Master Notes — Interstitial (Diffuse Parenchymal) Lung Disease

#### 5.1 Framework and Approach

ILD = a heterogeneous group causing **inflammation and/or fibrosis of the lung interstitium** → **restrictive** spirometry, **low DLCO**, and **fine "velcro" crackles**. The workup triangulates **HRCT pattern + serology + exposure/drug history**, discussed at an **ILD MDT**; biopsy only when the diagnosis remains unclear.

**Two anchors that solve most ILD questions:**

**(a) Zone of predominance:**

| Upper-zone fibrosis — **"CHARTS"** | Lower-zone fibrosis |
|---|---|
| **C**oal worker's pneumoconiosis / **C**ystic fibrosis | **I**diopathic pulmonary fibrosis |
| **H**ypersensitivity pneumonitis | Connective-tissue disease (RA, systemic sclerosis) |
| **A**nkylosing spondylitis | **A**sbestosis |
| **R**adiation | Drugs (amiodarone, methotrexate, nitrofurantoin, bleomycin) |
| **T**uberculosis / **S**arcoidosis / **S**ilicosis | |

**(b) UIP vs NSIP pattern:**
- **UIP (usual interstitial pneumonia)** — **peripheral, basal, reticulation with honeycombing and traction bronchiectasis, heterogeneous/patchy**; the pattern of **IPF** (and chronic HP, asbestosis, RA).
- **NSIP** — **more uniform ground-glass, less honeycombing, subpleural sparing**; associated with **connective-tissue disease**; steroid-responsive.

#### 5.2 Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis

Progressive fibrosing ILD of **unknown cause**, older patients, **UIP pattern**. Clinical: insidious exertional dyspnoea, dry cough, **fine bibasal velcro crackles, clubbing**; restrictive PFTs with low DLCO. Diagnosis by **HRCT UIP pattern** (MDT), excluding CTD/exposure. Poor prognosis (median survival ~3–5 years).

⚑ **Antifibrotics — a genuine change in management:** **pirfenidone** and **nintedanib** *slow the decline in FVC* (they don't reverse fibrosis) and are now standard for IPF and other **progressive fibrosing ILD phenotypes**. Steroids/immunosuppression **do not help IPF** (and can harm — the PANTHER trial) — a classic exam contrast with the CTD-associated/NSIP ILDs, which *are* steroid-responsive. Supportive: oxygen, pulmonary rehab, treat reflux, **lung transplant** in suitable candidates.

#### 5.3 Sarcoidosis

Multisystem **non-caseating granulomatous** disease, commonest in young adults (and, classically, higher incidence/severity in African-Caribbean patients). Lung is involved in >90%.
- **CXR staging:** 0 (normal), **1 = bilateral hilar lymphadenopathy (BHL) alone**, 2 = BHL + infiltrates, 3 = infiltrates alone, 4 = fibrosis.
- **Löfgren syndrome** = **BHL + erythema nodosum + arthralgia ± fever** — an **acute, good-prognosis** presentation, often self-limiting.
- Extra-pulmonary: skin (**lupus pernio**, erythema nodosum), eyes (uveitis), **hypercalcaemia** (macrophage 1α-hydroxylase → ↑vitamin D activation), cardiac (arrhythmia/heart block), neuro, parotid.
- Bloods: **raised serum ACE** (non-specific), **hypercalcaemia/hypercalciuria**; biopsy shows **non-caseating granulomas**. Management: many need **no treatment** (observe); **corticosteroids** for organ-threatening disease (eye, cardiac, neuro, hypercalcaemia, progressive lung).

#### 5.4 Hypersensitivity Pneumonitis and Pneumoconioses

**Hypersensitivity pneumonitis (extrinsic allergic alveolitis)** — immune reaction to inhaled organic antigens: **bird fancier's lung** (avian proteins), **farmer's lung** (thermophilic actinomycetes in mouldy hay). **Acute** (flu-like hours after exposure, reversible) vs **chronic** (upper/mid-zone fibrosis). **Discriminator: symptoms relate to exposure** — take an occupational/hobby/pet history.

**Pneumoconioses (inorganic dust):**

| Disease | Exposure | Signature |
|---|---|---|
| **Coal worker's pneumoconiosis** | Coal dust | Upper-zone nodules; **Caplan syndrome** (+ RA → nodules) |
| **Silicosis** | Sandblasting, mining, quarrying | Upper-zone nodules, **"eggshell" hilar node calcification**; ↑**TB** risk |
| **Asbestosis** | Asbestos (latency decades) | **Lower-zone** fibrosis; **pleural plaques** (benign marker of exposure); risk of **mesothelioma** and bronchial carcinoma |
| **Berylliosis** | Aerospace, electronics | Sarcoid-like granulomas |

*Discriminator:* **pleural plaques = exposure marker (benign)**; **asbestosis = the fibrosis**; **mesothelioma = the malignancy** — three different things from the same fibre.

#### 5.5 CTD-Associated and Drug-Induced ILD

Always screen for connective-tissue disease in new ILD (**RA, systemic sclerosis** [commonly NSIP], **dermatomyositis/anti-synthetase — anti-Jo-1, "mechanic's hands"**, SLE, Sjögren): symptoms, autoantibodies (ANA, ENA, RF/anti-CCP, myositis panel). **Drug-induced ILD** — the classic culprits: **amiodarone, methotrexate, nitrofurantoin, bleomycin** (and now several biologics/checkpoint inhibitors → pneumonitis). Management is to remove the cause and treat the (often steroid-responsive) inflammation.

#### Rapid-Recall Discriminators — Section 5

| Clue / question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Restrictive PFTs + low DLCO + velcro crackles | Interstitial lung disease |
| Upper-zone fibrosis | CHARTS (Coal, HP, AS, Radiation, TB/Sarcoid/Silicosis) |
| Lower-zone fibrosis | IPF, CTD, Asbestosis, drugs |
| Peripheral basal reticulation + honeycombing (UIP) | IPF (and asbestosis, chronic HP, RA) |
| Uniform ground-glass, subpleural sparing (NSIP) | CTD-associated ILD (steroid-responsive) |
| Drug that slows FVC decline in IPF | Pirfenidone / nintedanib ⚑ |
| Steroids **harmful** in this ILD | **IPF** (but helpful in CTD/NSIP/sarcoid) |
| Bilateral hilar lymphadenopathy + erythema nodosum + arthralgia | Löfgren syndrome (sarcoid, good prognosis) |
| Hypercalcaemia mechanism in sarcoid | Macrophage 1α-hydroxylase → ↑active vitamin D |
| Non-caseating granulomas + raised ACE | Sarcoidosis |
| Symptoms worse at work, better on holiday (with fibrosis) | Hypersensitivity pneumonitis / occupational |
| Eggshell hilar calcification + ↑TB risk | Silicosis |
| Pneumoconiosis + RA nodules | Caplan syndrome |
| Pleural plaques | Asbestos **exposure** marker (benign) |
| "Mechanic's hands", anti-Jo-1, myositis + ILD | Anti-synthetase syndrome |
