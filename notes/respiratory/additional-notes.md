## Additional Notes


### Respiratory (ANU) — lungs — Additional Notes

![](notes/images/respiratory-anu/img002.jpg)

![](notes/images/respiratory-anu/img003.jpg)

- RIGHT LUNG: 3 lobes
- LEFT LUNG: 2 lobes and a lingula
- TRACHEA extends from C6 to T4/5 and bifurcates into left/right main bronchus. The lowest tracheal ring is the carina
- Right lung is more common for inhaled foreign bodies (right main bronchus is wider, more vertical and shorter)
- WITHIN ROOT/HILUM: 1 pulmonary artery, 2 pulmonary veins, main bronchus, bronchial vessels, nerves, lymphatics
- 80% of respiratory epithelium is pseudostratified ciliated columnar epithelium, containing goblet cells

### Respiratory (ANU) — Recesses — Additional Notes

- ![](notes/images/respiratory-anu/img004.jpg)
The lungs don’t completely fill anterior/posterior/inferior regions of pleural cavities, creating recesses (two layers of parietal pleura become opposed)
- Potential spaces in which fluid can accumulate and be aspirated from
- Most clinically relevant: costodiaphragmatic recess
