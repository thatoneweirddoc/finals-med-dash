#### 11.1 Haematinics & marrow support

| Drug | Use | Watch |
|---|---|---|
| **Ferrous sulfate/fumarate (oral iron)** | Iron deficiency | GI upset, black stools, constipation; take with vitamin C, avoid with tea/PPIs; Hb rise ~20 g/L/month |
| **IV iron** (ferric carboxymaltose) | Intolerance/malabsorption, CKD, rapid need | Anaphylaxis (rare), staining at site |
| **Hydroxocobalamin (B12)** | B12 deficiency/PA | IM loading; treat **before/with** folate (avoid SACD) |
| **Folic acid** | Folate deficiency, pregnancy | Exclude B12 deficiency first |
| **Erythropoiesis-stimulating agents** (epoetin, darbepoetin) | Renal anaemia, some chemo anaemia | Ensure iron-replete; hypertension, thrombosis; avoid over-correcting Hb |
| **G-CSF (filgrastim)** | Post-chemo neutropenia, stem-cell mobilisation | Bone pain |
| **Iron chelators** (desferrioxamine, deferasirox) | Transfusional iron overload (thalassaemia) | Visual/auditory (desferrioxamine), renal (deferasirox) |

#### 11.2 Anticoagulants & antiplatelets

*(Mechanisms/reversal detailed in Section 5.5 — summarised here.)*

- **Unfractionated heparin** — IV, monitor **APTT**, reversed by **protamine**; use when rapid on/off needed (renal failure, peri-procedure).
- **LMWH (enoxaparin, dalteparin)** — subcut, predictable, no routine monitoring (anti-Xa in renal impairment/pregnancy/extremes of weight); VTE treatment/prophylaxis, ACS.
- **Warfarin** — vitamin K antagonist, **INR** monitoring, many interactions, **teratogenic**, initial procoagulant effect (bridge with heparin); reversal ⚑ **vitamin K 5–10 mg IV + 4-factor PCC (Beriplex) 50 IU/kg** (cap 100 kg; 25–50 IU/kg for lesser bleeding), **without FFP** — the 3-factor **Prothrombinex-VF** was discontinued around **June 2024**, and FFP was only ever needed to supply its missing factor VII, so "Prothrombinex + FFP" is the **old** answer (**Critical Care §3.14**). Still first-line for **mechanical valves and antiphospholipid syndrome** ⚑.
- **DOACs** — **dabigatran** (direct thrombin; reversal **idarucizumab**) and **apixaban/rivaroxaban/edoxaban** (direct Xa; reversal ⚑ **4-factor PCC (Beriplex)** — **andexanet alfa was removed from the ARTG on 20 May 2026**, so Australian Xa-inhibitor reversal is now PCC-based; **Critical Care §3.14**). No routine monitoring; **renally cleared** (caution/avoid in severe renal impairment); now first-line for most VTE/AF and much cancer-associated thrombosis ⚑; **avoid in mechanical valves and triple-positive APS**.
- **Fondaparinux** — synthetic Xa inhibitor (used in ACS, HIT).
- **Antiplatelets** — **aspirin** (COX-1 → ↓thromboxane), **P2Y12 inhibitors** (clopidogrel/ticagrelor/prasugrel); bleeding risk.
- **Thrombolytics** (alteplase/tenecteplase) — massive PE, stroke, STEMI; major bleeding risk.

#### 11.3 Cytotoxic chemotherapy

Cytotoxics damage rapidly dividing cells → **shared toxicities: myelosuppression (nadir ~7–14 days), mucositis, alopecia, nausea/vomiting, infertility, second malignancy, tumour lysis.** Learn the **class-specific signature toxicities** (a favourite written-paper item):

| Class | Examples | Mechanism | Signature toxicity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Alkylating agents** | Cyclophosphamide, ifosfamide | Cross-link DNA | **Haemorrhagic cystitis** (give **mesna**); infertility, secondary leukaemia |
| **Platinum agents** | Cisplatin, carboplatin, oxaliplatin | DNA cross-links | **Nephrotoxicity, ototoxicity, peripheral neuropathy** (cisplatin); **cold-induced neuropathy** (oxaliplatin) |
| **Antimetabolites** | Methotrexate, 5-FU, capecitabine, gemcitabine | Block DNA synthesis | MTX: mucositis, myelosuppression, hepatic/pulmonary — **folinic acid rescue**; 5-FU: mucositis, diarrhoea, coronary spasm |
| **Anthracyclines** | Doxorubicin, daunorubicin | Topoisomerase II, free radicals | **Cardiotoxicity** (cumulative, dose-limiting — monitor LVEF); red urine |
| **Vinca alkaloids** | Vincristine, vinblastine | Inhibit microtubule assembly | **Peripheral neuropathy** (vincristine); **vincristine is fatal if given intrathecally** ⚑ |
| **Taxanes** | Paclitaxel, docetaxel | Stabilise microtubules | Peripheral neuropathy, hypersensitivity, myelosuppression |
| **Topoisomerase inhibitors** | Etoposide, irinotecan | Block topoisomerase | Irinotecan: diarrhoea; myelosuppression |
| **Bleomycin** | — | DNA strand breaks | **Pulmonary fibrosis**; minimal myelosuppression |

⚑ **Vincristine intrathecal error** is a never-event that has killed patients — vinca alkaloids are IV only.

#### 11.4 Targeted & immune therapy

| Agent / class | Target | Key toxicity / note |
|---|---|---|
| **Imatinib, dasatinib, nilotinib** | BCR-ABL1 (CML), c-KIT (GIST) | Oedema, cytopenias; transformed CML prognosis ⚑ |
| **Osimertinib/erlotinib** | EGFR (NSCLC) | Rash, diarrhoea |
| **Rituximab** | CD20 (B-cell lymphoma/CLL, autoimmune) | Infusion reactions, hepatitis B reactivation, infection |
| **Trastuzumab (Herceptin)** | HER2 (breast, gastric) | **Cardiotoxicity** (esp. with anthracyclines) — monitor LVEF |
| **Bevacizumab** | VEGF | Hypertension, bleeding, poor wound healing, GI perforation |
| **Cetuximab** | EGFR (RAS-WT colorectal, H&N) | Acneiform rash, hypomagnesaemia |
| **Checkpoint inhibitors** (pembrolizumab/nivolumab PD-1; atezolizumab PD-L1; ipilimumab CTLA-4) | Immune checkpoints | **Immune-related adverse events** — colitis, pneumonitis, hepatitis, thyroiditis/hypophysitis — treat with **steroids** ⚑ |
| **CAR-T cell therapy** | CD19 (refractory B-cell) | **Cytokine release syndrome** (fever/hypotension — tocilizumab), neurotoxicity ⚑ |
| **Endocrine — tamoxifen** | ER antagonist (breast tissue) | VTE, endometrial cancer, hot flushes (pre-menopausal) |
| **Endocrine — aromatase inhibitors** (anastrozole/letrozole) | Block peripheral oestrogen synthesis | Osteoporosis, arthralgia (**post**-menopausal breast) |
| **Androgen deprivation** (GnRH agonists — goserelin; anti-androgens) | Testosterone suppression (prostate) | Hot flushes, bone loss, metabolic; **initial tumour flare** with GnRH agonists |

#### 11.5 Supportive drugs

- **Antiemetics by emetogenic risk:** low → metoclopramide/domperidone; moderate–high → **5-HT₃ antagonists (ondansetron)** + **dexamethasone** ± **NK1 antagonist (aprepitant)**; anticipatory → lorazepam.
- **Tumour-lysis prophylaxis:** **allopurinol** (xanthine oxidase inhibitor — prevents urate formation) or **rasburicase** (recombinant urate oxidase — breaks down existing urate; **avoid in G6PD deficiency**), with IV hydration.
- **Bone-targeted:** **bisphosphonates** (zoledronic acid — hypercalcaemia of malignancy, myeloma/metastatic bone disease; watch osteonecrosis of the jaw, renal) and **denosumab** (RANKL antibody).
- **Growth factors:** G-CSF (neutropenia), ESAs (anaemia), TPO agonists (ITP).
- **Analgesia:** WHO ladder (Section 8.5); **allopurinol interacts with azathioprine/6-MP** (blocks their metabolism → toxicity — reduce dose).

#### 11.6 Prescribing safety

High-yield safety points, each an exam favourite:
- **Neutropenic sepsis:** never delay broad-spectrum antibiotics for the blood count. ⚑
- **Methotrexate is dosed WEEKLY, not daily** — daily dosing is a fatal error; co-prescribe **folic acid** (on a different day) and monitor FBC/LFTs; **folinic acid (leucovorin) rescue** for high-dose/toxicity.
- **Vincristine is intravenous only** — intrathecal administration is fatal. ⚑
- **Warfarin** — check interactions and INR; **teratogenic**.
- **Chemotherapy is dosed by body-surface area / AUC (carboplatin)** — verify calculations; cytotoxics need safe handling.
- **Allopurinol + azathioprine/6-mercaptopurine** — dangerous interaction (reduce thiopurine dose).
- **Trastuzumab/anthracyclines** — monitor cardiac function.
- **ESAs** — do not over-correct Hb (thrombosis risk); ensure iron-replete first.

BENIGN (70%)

- Infectious granuloma (histoplasmosis, coccidiomycosis, TB, atypical mycobacteria)
- Other infections (bacterial abscess, PCP, aspergilloma)
- Benign neoplasms (hamartoma, lipoma, fibroma)
- Vascular (AV malformation, pulmonary varix)
- Developmental (bronchogenic cyst)
- Inflammatory (granulomatosis with polyangiitis, rheumatoid nodule, sarcoidosis)
- Other (infarct, pseudotumour, rounded atelectasis, lymph nodes, amyloidoma)
