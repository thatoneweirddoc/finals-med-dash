## Reproductive, Metabolic & Multi-Gland Endocrinology


### Endocrine Master Notes — Reproductive, Metabolic & Multi-Gland Endocrinology

#### 10.1 Female Reproductive Endocrinology

##### Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS)
Commonest endocrine disorder in women of reproductive age; a state of **hyperandrogenism + insulin resistance**.
- **Rotterdam criteria** — diagnosis needs **2 of 3**: (1) **oligo/anovulation**, (2) **clinical or biochemical hyperandrogenism** (hirsutism, acne; raised free testosterone), (3) **polycystic ovaries on ultrasound** — after excluding mimics (CAH, Cushing's, androgen-secreting tumour, hyperprolactinaemia, thyroid disease). ⚑
- **Associated:** obesity, insulin resistance/impaired glucose tolerance/T2DM, dyslipidaemia, subfertility, **endometrial hyperplasia** risk (from unopposed oestrogen).
- **Biochemistry clue:** raised **LH:FSH ratio** (classic but not required), raised free testosterone with **normal/low SHBG**, normal/mildly raised androgens (a *very* high testosterone suggests a tumour instead).
- **Management:** weight loss/lifestyle first; **combined oral contraceptive** for menstrual regulation/hyperandrogenism; **metformin** for metabolic features; **letrozole** (first-line) or clomifene for ovulation induction; ensure endometrial protection (regular withdrawal bleeds).

##### Amenorrhoea and hirsutism
- **Primary amenorrhoea** (no menarche): Turner's, constitutional delay, imperforate hymen, androgen insensitivity, GnRH deficiency (Kallmann's — with anosmia).
- **Secondary amenorrhoea:** **pregnancy first**, then hypothalamic (low weight/stress/exercise), **hyperprolactinaemia**, PCOS, thyroid disease, premature ovarian insufficiency, Sheehan's, Asherman's.
- **Rapid virilisation** (deep voice, clitoromegaly, rapid hirsutism) → **androgen-secreting tumour or CAH**, not PCOS.

##### Menopause
Cessation of ovarian function → oestrogen deficiency: vasomotor symptoms, urogenital atrophy, mood/sleep change, accelerated bone loss. **HRT** relieves symptoms (weigh VTE/breast-cancer risk; give a progestogen with oestrogen if the uterus is present).

#### 10.2 Male Hypogonadism and Gynaecomastia

| | Primary (hypergonadotrophic) | Secondary (hypogonadotrophic) |
|---|---|---|
| Problem | Testis | Pituitary/hypothalamus |
| Testosterone | Low | Low |
| **LH/FSH** | **High** | **Low/normal** |
| Causes | **Klinefelter's (47,XXY)**, mumps orchitis, trauma, chemo | Hypopituitarism, **Kallmann's** (+anosmia), hyperprolactinaemia, obesity, opioids, steroids |

**Gynaecomastia** = benign glandular breast tissue in a male from a **raised oestrogen:androgen ratio**: physiological (neonatal, puberty, elderly), drugs (**spironolactone, digoxin, cimetidine, antipsychotics, cannabis, anti-androgens**), liver disease, hyperthyroidism, hypogonadism, and — important not to miss — **testicular/hCG-secreting tumours**. Distinguish from fatty pseudogynaecomastia and exclude malignancy if unilateral/hard/fixed.

#### 10.3 Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia and Neuroendocrine Tumours

**MEN syndromes** — autosomal dominant; learn the components (very high yield):

| Syndrome | Gene | Components |
|---|---|---|
| **MEN 1** ("**3 Ps**") | **MEN1** (menin) | **Parathyroid** hyperplasia (commonest/first), **Pancreatic** islet tumours (gastrinoma/insulinoma), **Pituitary** adenoma |
| **MEN 2A** | **RET** | **Medullary thyroid carcinoma**, **Phaeochromocytoma**, **Parathyroid** hyperplasia |
| **MEN 2B** | **RET** | Medullary thyroid carcinoma, phaeochromocytoma, **mucosal neuromas + marfanoid habitus** (no parathyroid disease) |

*Memory: MEN1 = "3 Ps" (Parathyroid, Pancreas, Pituitary); MEN2A = medullary + phaeo + parathyroid; MEN2B = medullary + phaeo + **mucosal neuromas/marfanoid** (Ps become "2 Ms").* **All MEN2 subtypes → medullary thyroid carcinoma** — RET-positive relatives are offered **prophylactic thyroidectomy**; always **exclude phaeochromocytoma before any surgery**.

**Carcinoid / neuroendocrine tumours:**
- **Carcinoid syndrome** (flushing, diarrhoea, wheeze, right-sided valvular heart disease) occurs when a serotonin-secreting NET **metastasises to the liver** (bypassing first-pass metabolism) or is bronchial. Diagnose with **urinary 5-HIAA** and chromogranin A; localise with somatostatin-receptor imaging (Ga-DOTATATE). Treat with **somatostatin analogues (octreotide)**; **carcinoid crisis** is precipitated by handling/anaesthesia → octreotide cover.
- Other functional pancreatic NETs: **insulinoma** (hypoglycaemia), **gastrinoma** (Zollinger–Ellison → refractory peptic ulcers/diarrhoea), **VIPoma** (watery diarrhoea), **glucagonoma** (necrolytic migratory erythema, diabetes).

#### 10.4 Obesity and Metabolic Syndrome

- **Metabolic syndrome:** clustering of **central obesity, hypertension, dyslipidaemia (high triglycerides/low HDL), and dysglycaemia/insulin resistance** — a cardiovascular and diabetes risk state.
- **Obesity management:** lifestyle first; **incretin-based pharmacotherapy** (GLP-1 RA **semaglutide**, GIP/GLP-1 **tirzepatide**) now has a major role and substantial weight-loss efficacy ⚑; **bariatric surgery** for higher BMI thresholds (typically BMI ≥40, or ≥35 with comorbidity; lower thresholds considered in certain groups) with durable weight loss and diabetes remission.

#### Rapid-Recall Discriminators — Section 10

| Clue / question | Answer |
|---|---|
| PCOS diagnostic framework | **Rotterdam — 2 of 3** (oligo-ovulation, hyperandrogenism, polycystic ovaries) |
| First-line ovulation induction in PCOS | **Letrozole** |
| Very high testosterone + rapid virilisation | **Androgen-secreting tumour** (not PCOS) |
| First test in secondary amenorrhoea | **Pregnancy test** |
| Hypogonadism + anosmia | **Kallmann syndrome** |
| Male hypogonadism with **high** LH/FSH | **Primary** (e.g. Klinefelter's 47,XXY) |
| Drug causes of gynaecomastia | Spironolactone, digoxin, cimetidine, antipsychotics, cannabis |

| MEN 2B distinguishing feature | **Mucosal neuromas + marfanoid** (no parathyroid) |
| Gene in MEN 2 | **RET** |
| Always exclude before MEN2 surgery | **Phaeochromocytoma** |
| Carcinoid syndrome needs | **Liver metastases** (or bronchial primary) |
| Test for carcinoid | **Urinary 5-HIAA** (+ chromogranin A) |
| Refractory peptic ulcers + diarrhoea | **Gastrinoma (Zollinger–Ellison)** |
