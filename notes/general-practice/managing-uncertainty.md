## Managing Uncertainty


### General Practice (ANU) — Managing Uncertainty

#### ICE

IDEAS, CONCERNS, EXPECTATIONS

#### Murtagh’s diagnostic model (PROMPT)

- P- What is the probability diagnosis?
- R- What serious disorders must not be missed? Teach patient about red flags – “safety netting”.
- O- What conditions are often missed (pitfalls)?
- M- Could this patient have one of the ‘masquerades’ in medical practice? depression, diabetes, UTI, CTD, drugs, iatrogenic, anemia, thyroid disorder, spinal dysfunction
- T- Is this patient trying to tell me something else? domestic violence, bereavement, workplace issues, PTSD
