## Illness and illness behavior


### General Practice (ANU) — Illness and illness behavior

- DISEASE: An objective, pathological process, a deviation from a biological norm
- ILLNESS: A subjective feeling of being unwell, an internal, personal experience
- SICKNESS: A social role/status
- ILLNESS BEHAVIOUR: Any activity undertaken for the purpose of defining that illness and seeking relief from it

#### Stages of the illness experience

- Symptom Experience
- Assumption of sick role
- Medical care contact
- Dependent patient role
- Recovery and rehabilitation

#### Illness behaviour

- Ignore it and it’ll go away
- Non-medical self-care: diet, exercise, rest
- Lay consultation: family, friends, google
- Medical self-care: natural remedies, OTC drugs
- Seeking professional help
