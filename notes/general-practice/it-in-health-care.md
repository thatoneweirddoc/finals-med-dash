## IT in health care


### General Practice (ANU) — IT in health care

#### Benefits:

- Efficient and safer medication management
- Patient information management (referrals, progress notes, etc.)
- Diagnostic test management
- Appointment systems (recalls, reminders)
- Secure communication
- Chronic disease management

#### Potential problems:

- Data security
- Overreliance on technology
- Power outages
- Technology driving the consult
- Pop-up fatigue

#### Strategies to maintain doctor-pateint relationship:

- Maintain eye contact for first few minutes of consultation
- Tell patient what you are doing on computer
- Always give attention back to patient if they start talking as you’re typing
- Share info on the screen with patient
