Depression

- MEDICATION:
- Start low dose and trial for 3-4 weeks at an adequate dose
- Allow washout for 2-4 days before starting new medication
- SSRI have a flat dose-response curve whereas higher doses of TCA may be helpful
- Psychiatrist advice (diagnosis e.g. bipolar, medication advice e.g. lithium/lamotrigine)
- SSRI toxicity: hypoNa+, altered mental status, autonomic instability, neuromuscular abnormalities

#### post-natal depression

- EDINBURGH PND SCALE
- DEPRESSION IN PREGNANCY: ↑ risk of premature delivery, low birth weight, gestational HTN, intrauterine death
- DURING PREGNANCY:
- SSRI paroxetine (heart malformations) contraindicated
- SSRIs/TCAs cause “jittery baby” (neonatal adaptation syndrome) but this is not a contraindication
- Venlafaxine not associated with congenital abnormality, but neonatal withdrawal reported

#### Beck’s Theories for depression

- THE COGNITIVE TRIAD (OF NEGATIVE AUTOMATIC THINKING)
- NEGATIVE SELF-SCHEMAS
- ERRORS IN LOGIC

- Illogical thinking processes
- Arbitrary interference
- Selective abstraction: focusing on a single aspect of a situation and ignoring others
- Magnification:  exaggerating the importance of undesirable events
- Minimisation: underplaying significance of a positive event
- Overgeneralisation
- Personalisation: attributing the negative feelings of others to yourself
