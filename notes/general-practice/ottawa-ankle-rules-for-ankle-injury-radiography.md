## Ottawa Ankle Rules – for Ankle Injury Radiography


### General Practice (ANU) — Ottawa Ankle Rules – for Ankle Injury Radiography

An ANKLE X-RAY is only required if there is any pain in the malleolar zone and any of:

- Bone tenderness at posterior edge or tip of lateral malleolus OR
- Bone tenderness at posterior edge or tip of medial malleolus OR
- Inability to take 4 complete steps both immediately and in ED

A FOOT X-RAY is only required if there is any pain in the midfoot zone and any of:

- Bone tenderness at base of 5th metatarsal OR
- Bone tenderness at the navicular OR
- Inability to take 4 complete steps both immediately and in ED
