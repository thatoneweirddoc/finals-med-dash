## Clinical Handover


### General Practice (ANU) — Clinical Handover

- I: IDENTIFY – your role and the patient
- S: SITUATION – immediate clinical situation of the patient
- B: BACKGROUND – history to the patient’s clinical situation
- A: ASSESSMENT – of patient
- R: RECOMMENDATIONS – for further actions

BARRIERS TO EFFECTIVE HANDOVER: content omissions and errors, incomplete or unclear communication, lack of shared understanding, interruptions and distractions, effect of hierarchy and power, lack of training, busyness
