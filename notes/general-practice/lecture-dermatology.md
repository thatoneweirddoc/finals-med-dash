## lecture: dermatology


### General Practice (ANU) — lecture: dermatology

#### Skin

- Largest organ in the body
- Shield effect: temperature change, UV, physical and chemical trauma, infection
- Factory for Vit D: Ca2+ to bones
- Sensory input for brain
- Completely free movement

#### Structure and Function of Skin

- STRATUM CORNEUM: protection, fluid conservation, UV
- EPIDERMIS (KERATINOCYTES): produce keratin, protection, replication
- DERMIS (COLLAGEN AND ELASTIN): strength and support, elasticity
- SUBCUTANEOUS FAT: cushioning and thermoregulation
- HAIR: touch, protection, cosmetic, thermoregulation
- SEBACEOUS GLANDS: moisturizing grease
- ECCRINE GLANDS: thermoregulation
- APOCRINE GLANDS
- SPECIALISED CELLS: melanocytes (UV protection), Langerhans (immune surveillance), merkel cells, meiseners, Pacinian, nerve endings
- BLOOD VESSELS AND LYMPHATICS

#### Dermatology History

- HPI: duration, site of onset, change in appearance, symptoms (local, systemic)
- PMHX: skin diseases, systemic diseases, allergies, previous/current treatments and responses
- FHX: genetic e.g. atopy. Infections, infestations e.g. impetigo, scabies
- OCCUPATION AND HOBBIES: e.g. outdoor worker (skin cancer)

#### Examination

- BRIGHT LIGHT AND MAGNIFICENT
- SITE AND DISTRIBUTION
- CHARACTERISTICS OF INDIVIDUAL LESION: type, size, shape, outline, colour, surface
- PALPATION: superficial or deep
- SECONDARY SITES: nails, fingers, wrists with scabies

#### Investigations

- DERMOSCOPY
- BIOPSY: punch, shave, curette, incision
- ALLERGY TESTS: prick tests, patch tests
- SCRAPINGS AND CLIPPINGS for tinea, scabies
- BLOODS AND URINE: lupus
- IMAGING: X-rays, US, scans for sarcoid, tumours etc.

#### Describing a rash

- COLOUR: erythema, pigmentation, purpura
- SHAPE: discoid, annular, serpiginous
- SURFACE CHANGES: crust, scale, lichenification (thickened skin)
- MACULE: flat circumscribed area of discolouration
- PAPULE: small lump &lt;5mm
- NODULE: larger lump &gt;5mm
- PLAQUE: circumscribed area of elevated skin
- EROSION: area of loss of epidermis
- EXCORIATION: ulceration due to scratching
- FISSURE: crack through epidermis into dermis
- ULCER: loss of epidermis, some dermis ± possibly fat
- FLUID FILLED LESIONS: vesicle (&lt;5mm), bulla (&gt;5mm), pustule (visible accumulation of pus)
- TELANGIECTASIA: small blood vessels on skin surface
- PETECHIAE: small dots of blood in skin
- PURPURA: larger patches of blood
- CRUST: accumulated dried exudate
- SCALE: visible flakes of shedding keratin
- LICHENIFICATION: thickened skin secondary to scratching

#### Topical skin treatments

- TYPES: barrier protection, moisturizing, drying, keratolytic, anti-inflammatory, anti-infective, anti-proliferative
- SYSTEMIC: Abx, antifungals, antivirals, anti-inflammatory, anti-tumour, anti-histamines, steroids, immunosuppressants
- CREAMS need a preservative; used for subacute conditions
- OINTMENTS ARE EMOLLIENTS. Occlusive. Used for chronic conditions
- PAINTS AND LOTIONS: drying effect. Best for acute weeping rashes and hair bearing areas
- GELS: clear, greaseless, dry when applied. Good for face
- POWDERS: drying effect. Rashes in groin, under breasts etc
- PASTES: combination of ointment and power. Less greasy than ointment. Stick longer

#### Skin diseases

- DERMATITIS
- Exogenous: external factors e.g. allergic, irritant
- Endogenous: genetically pre-determined e.g. atopic, discoid, seborrheic, asteatotic
- INFLAMMATORY: psoriasis, urticaria/hives, acne, rosacea, lichen planus, pityriasis rosea, miliaria (heat), pregnancy
- INHERITED: neurofibromatosis, tuberous sclerosis
- PIGMENTARY: vitiligo, pityriasis alba
- HAIR AND NAILS: alopecia areata, onycholysis, paronychia, ingrown toenails, subungual haematoma, fungal infection, melanoma
- VASCULAR: venous ulcers, vasculitis
- AUTOIMMUNE: LE, scleroderma, bullous disease
- PRURITUS (ITCH): skin problems, systemic problems
- SYSTEMIC DISEASES AND THE SKIN: xanthomas, acanthosis nigricans
- CUTANEOUS DRUG REACTIONS: many and varied
- HYPERSENSITIVITY: erythema multiforme, vasculitis, Sweets Syndrome
- TUMOUR: benign (cysts, moles, skin tags), malignant (BCC, SCC, melanoma)
- INFECTIONS
- Bacterial: cellulitis, impetigo
- Fungal: thrush, tinea
- Viral: measles, herpes, warts
- Parasites: scabies, nits, lice
