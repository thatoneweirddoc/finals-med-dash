## Breaking Bad News - SPIKES


### General Practice (ANU) — Breaking Bad News - SPIKES

S – SETTING UP THE INTERVIEW

- Arrange for some privacy and sit down
- Involve significant others

P – PERCEPTION:

- Assess patient’s perception of their medical situation by asking open-ended questions
- Based on this information, you can rectify any misconceptions

I – INVITATION:

- While a majority of patients express a desire for full information about their diagnosis, some patients do not have a psychological coping mechanism
- E.g. Would you like me to give you all the results or spend more time on the treatment plan?

K – KNOWLEDGE

- Giving knowledge and information to the patient
- Warning the patient that bad news is coming may lessen the shock that can follow

E – EMOTIONS

- Addressing the patients emotions with empathetic responses

S – STRATEGY AND SUMMARY

- Before discussing a treatment plan, ask if the patient is ready for such a discussion
- Discuss a clear plan for the future to relieve anxiety and uncertainty
