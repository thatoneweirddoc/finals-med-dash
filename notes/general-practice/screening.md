## screening


### General Practice (ANU) — screening

#### Ideal Screening Test

- PROBLEM: important, recognisable latent phase, good understanding of process
- TEST: acceptable, validated, easy to perform, agreed values in population, highly sensitive
- TREATMENT: effective, agreement on who to treat
- OUTCOME: evidence of morbidity/mortality improvement, cost benefit, risk benefit

#### Potential harms of screening/Dangers of screening

- Anxiety
- Over-diagnosis
- False positives and negatives
- Complications for unnecessary procedures
- Cost

#### Screening Vs Diagnostic Tests

- PURPOSE: detect disease indicators Vs establish presence/absence of disease
- TARGET POPULATION: large numbers of asymptomatic people Vs symptomatic people
- TEST METHOD: simple, generally non-invasive Vs possible invasive
- POSITIVE RESULT THRESHOLD: lowered/chosen for sensitivity Vs higher/chosen for specificity
- POSITIVE RESULT: indicates suspicion of disease Vs definite diagnosis
- COST: cheap individually, though large numbers are performed Vs higher individual costs
