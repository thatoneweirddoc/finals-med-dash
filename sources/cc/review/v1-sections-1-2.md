## Adjudications requested

**1. Post-arrest SpO₂ target: §1 is right, §5.3 is wrong.** ANZCOR 11.6.1 (Feb 2026) and 11.7 (Sep 2024) both give **94–98%** (88–92% in hypercapnic respiratory failure) after ROSC — verbatim. That is an Australian recommendation, so §5.3's claim that 94–98% is "the UK/BTS figure" and that the 92–96 vs 94–98 gap is "the only genuine Australia-versus-UK split in the oxygen targets" cannot stand. The correct framing: TSANZ 92–96% is the general acute medical band; ANZCOR 94–98% is a context-specific post-arrest band that happens to coincide with BTS and ERC. Fix §5.3 (finding above); leave §1.9 and §1.12 untouched.

**2. Ventilator rate in the intubated asthmatic: both are defensible, neither is labelled.** §1.12's **<10/min** is a verbatim ANZCOR 11.10 quote and applies to arrest/peri-arrest asthma. §5.11's **≈10–14/min** describes ongoing ICU ventilation, but its upper bound is unsupported — the standard range is ≈8–12/min or lower. Fix §5.11's number and add the context label to both (finding above).

**3. Between the Flags urine output — the flagged error does not exist; the note is correct.** Yellow <100 mL/4 h and Red <200 mL/8 h *are* the published NSW criteria, verified on the CEC Standard Adult General Observation Chart and on the SESLHD 2025 registrar orientation reproduction. They are the same *rate* by design: escalation between the zones is by **duration of documented oliguria** (4 h vs 8 h), plus the parallel <0.5 mL/kg/h IDC criterion. Do not "correct" this table. The only defect is the omitted IDC clause and the missing "persistent for" framing, which is what makes the logic look broken (finding above).

**4. MAP targets: three different numbers, three different guidelines, no inconsistency.** Post-ROSC **MAP ≥60–65 or SBP >100** is verbatim ANZCOR 11.7 (Sep 2024). Septic shock **≥65** is the Sepsis-3/SSC target. Brain-death testing **SBP ≥90 or MAP ≥60** is an ANZICS testing precondition, a different kind of number entirely (a floor for valid testing, not a resuscitation target). Leave all three as written. If anything, §1.9 could add "(ANZCOR 11.7)" inline so the reader sees why the post-ROSC figure is a range.

**5. Reperfusion divergence: real, and both sides are stated correctly.** ACSQHC ACS Clinical Care Standard (2014, reviewed 2019, still not updated) — ECG interpreted within 10 min of first emergency clinical contact; primary PCI where FMC-to-balloon is anticipated <90 min; fibrinolysis before or within 30 min of hospital presentation. NHFA/CSANZ 2025 — primary PCI <60 min FMC at a PCI-capable centre, <90 min at a non-PCI-capable centre. Both verified. No change needed.

**6. Qld vs NSW olanzapine: real, and both figures are correct.** Queensland Health QH-GDL-438 v5.0 (21 Oct 2024) — olanzapine 10 mg PO stat, max 30 mg by any route/24 h. NSW ACI adult ECAT ABD protocol — olanzapine 5 mg oral, once only. Verified on both sites. The sequencing rule the brief asked about is **not stated in either direction** anywhere in §2.9 — it is simply absent, which is the dangerous finding above. It has not been stated backwards.
